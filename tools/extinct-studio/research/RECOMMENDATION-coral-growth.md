# RETRACTED — coral idea was not a market selection

This file is retained as an audit of a workflow mistake. The coral candidate was
found through GitHub repository discovery before a fresh YouTube same-format market
pass. That ordering was wrong for this channel.

## Decision

- Do not treat *A Coral's Life in Cross-Section* as the first Extinct video.
- Do not write its script, generate voice, build a proof, render it, package it,
  or present it as a YouTube launch candidate.
- Do not treat the repository's README, dataset size, or license claim as market
  evidence.

## What was wrong

The repository may be useful later for scientific provenance or rights research,
but it answered a production/data question, not the real question:

> What promise, topic gap, title/thumbnail language, and long-form story pattern
> are working in the actual YouTube market for this channel?

That question must come first.

## Correct replacement

1. Fresh YouTube channel/cohort research.
2. Same-format long-form comparison.
3. Market gap and viewer-promise report.
4. New candidate topics derived from that report.
5. Primary evidence and rights ledger for each candidate.
6. Owner selection.
7. Eight-to-twelve-second moving proof.
8. Only then script, voice, render, QA, and private handoff.

No topic is currently selected. The mistake is corrected in
`long-form-shortlist-2026-11-21.md` and the JSON slate is marked retracted.

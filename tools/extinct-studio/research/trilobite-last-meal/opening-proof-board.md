# Opening proof board — research only

**Status:** planning board, not a script or final frame.  
**Source:** P1, Kraft et al. 2023, Nature.  
**Asset rule:** all shapes are original; no Nature figure, scan render, specimen
photo, paleoart, or third-party thumbnail is reused.

## Four-beat proof test

| Beat | Visual proof | Claim boundary | Phone-size test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Fossil | Original stylized articulated trilobite silhouette, age and taxon label | specimen and age are published facts | taxon may shrink; keep “465 million years” large |
| 02 Cutaway | Original body cutaway with a continuous gut path and shell fragments | shell-rich gut fill is fossil evidence | green gut path and yellow fragments must stay distinct |
| 03 Mechanism | Flow from shell survival to feeding interpretation | “light crusher/chance feeder” is a paper-supported inference | separate observation and inference labels |
| 04 Limit | Explicit near-neutral/alkaline inference plus “not direct pH” | one specimen does not define every trilobite | limitation must not disappear below the fold |

## Why this passes the first proof test

The central reveal is not dependent on a dramatic reconstructed animal. A viewer
can understand the evidence chain using only a body outline, a cutaway, shell
fragments, arrows, and source-boundary labels. The board therefore supports a
future native visual proof without importing the paper's scan render.

## What is deliberately absent

- no final narration;
- no timing tied to a voice track;
- no soundtrack, Foley, or captions;
- no final title or thumbnail;
- no claim that the current board is a finished opening;
- no rights-cleared source media.

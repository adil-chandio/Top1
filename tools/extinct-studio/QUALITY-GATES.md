# Extinct Studio quality gates

The gates are designed to prevent avoidable failure, not to manufacture a viral
prediction. A project may move forward only when the current gate is evidenced.

## Gate 0 — Idea

- One-sentence viewer promise.
- Audience and viewer question identified.
- Short, long, or both format selected.
- At least two hook directions.
- No historical subject or release candidate silently reused.
- Idea score is at least 80 **and** evidence, proof, and rights blockers are clear.

## Gate 1 — Research

- Same-format cohort sampled, not just a list of popular channels.
- Dates, runtime, format, title promise, and source URL recorded.
- Views treated as descriptive observations, not causal forecasts.
- Primary scientific sources and limitations recorded.
- Reference footage is discovery evidence only unless separately cleared.

## Gate 2 — Claims and rights

- Every on-screen factual claim has a source ID.
- Claim type is marked: observed, measured, published, derived, modelled,
  illustrative, hypothesis, or limitation.
- Illustrative/modelled claims carry an explicit limitation.
- Every output asset has a source, license, status, and commercial-use decision.
- Unknown or pending rights block production.

## Gate 3 — Story and voice

- Hook matches the actual visual promise.
- Narration and picture notes are separate.
- Script is locked before final timing.
- Voice consent/rights are verified.
- Captions come from locked narration, not an unverified transcript.

## Gate 4 — Moving proof

- Eight to twelve seconds of actual native motion.
- Contact, scale, mechanism, or other central action is legible.
- Frame count equals duration × FPS.
- Phone-size review passes.
- Human review passes.
- No third-party source footage is being used as production footage.

## Gate 5 — Full picture and sound

- Render manifest pins renderer, shader/asset hashes, resolution, and FPS.
- Shot changes invalidate the affected cache rather than silently reusing stale
  plates.
- Voice, Foley, score, and bed do not mask each other.
- Mix is measured before and after the delivery encode.
- Mono/phone listening review passes.

## Gate 6 — Delivery QA

- Full decode succeeds.
- CFR clock and frame count pass.
- No black/frozen/duplicate frame failure in the inspected output.
- Captions are ordered, timed, and safe on a phone.
- Visual review and audio review both pass.
- SHA-256 identifies the exact delivered file.

## Gate 7 — Owner acceptance and private Studio handoff

- Owner acceptance names the accepted scope.
- The uploaded file hash matches the accepted delivery hash.
- Visibility is private.
- No schedule or publish action is implied by the handoff.
- Publication remains an owner-manual decision with a separate receipt.

## Post-publication learning

If the owner publishes, Studio metrics may be imported through a read-only,
redacted path. The result is a hypothesis update, not a claim that one upload
proved a causal formula. Keep format cohorts and calendar-age windows separate.

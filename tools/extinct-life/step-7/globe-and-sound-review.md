## 1. Sound: preserve the flow, not the sting

The restored Investigation and Sound Redesign MP4s match the handoff hashes, and the two AAC payloads match each other. So the picture edit did not introduce a new mix. Decoded frames were checked against the cue clock; the dry narration, non-voice bed and foreground Foley were compared separately. The reconstructed stem hashes match the original receipt. These are signal and timing findings, not a headphone audition or listening approval.

## 3. Globe: keep the new evidence edit

Compared the two original opening exports, Sound Redesign and Investigation frames at matching timecodes. **00:04.25 → 00:07.21:** in Opening 3D v2 the coastlines move relative to the silhouette before the two pins and the curved distance line settle. **00:10.75:** the rewind is a curved surface sweep, not a line sliding over a rectangle. **00:12.33 → 00:16.71:** the model pins and ancient distance settle on the same sphere. Investigation makes the captions and same-scale comparison more immediate, but loses that spatial motion.

- **00:04–00:18:** keep the print-match hook, then show the round outline clearly before moving in. Ease the orbit toward the Atlantic. Reveal anchored pins, labels, and the rounded separation in that order, using the current voice/cue clock. Make one restrained curved surface reveal into the independent 125 Ma snapshot; settle both landmarks before the next measurement. Use Investigation's simpler labels and legible contrast. The line is a regional measure, never a direction of travel. Mark **125 Ma as a model snapshot, not a print date**.
- **03:34** and **04:55**: reuse the same turning-sphere language for the Brazil and ancient-land locators, easing to a read rather than spinning continuously. Keep specific evidence insets, field scenes and science qualifications readable.
- **06:52–06:59:** return to the same modern Atlantic framing. Then cut out of the globe exactly where Investigation does: back to the two prints, then the two moving rock blocks at **07:04**. The old full cut stayed on the globe through the answer; the Investigation version explains it better. Keep the close and credits untouched.

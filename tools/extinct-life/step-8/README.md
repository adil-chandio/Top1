# Atlantic Tracks — Cinema Edition

A full-film revision of the **Investigation Cut**. The archive is verified at load and never edited. The 24 fps picture grid, 48 kHz dry English voice, movable/optional English captions, script, and nine chapters are the timebase. The finished output is one MP4 at `deliverables/extinct-life-step-8/Atlantic-Tracks-Cinema-Edition.mp4` (with a matching caption sidecar).

## What changed

- **Cartography throughout:** curving irradiance and atmosphere on a moving sphere, pinned labels, regional separation measured on the sphere, two independent epoch snapshots revealed with an on-sphere wipe. The same modern scale and camera return before the final question. The partly coloured map region is not a surveyed boundary or migration route.
- **Field and macro:** new camera paths and consistent materials from the close impressions to the cutaways; directional, depth-dependent water; different skies and wet banks; world-space vegetation, solid loose chips and filmic highlight roll-off. The foot releases after load and changes the bed; the stamp demonstrations use a common tool with wet and dry responses and separate release/slump anchors.
- **Evidence edit:** field counts lead into the broken record and photogrammetry, then the ~69 cm impression (not an animal dimension). The neighbouring materials, a distinct sediment front, grain contacts and strata precede the wide age assignments and reconstruction. Cutaways and the coelacanth are original illustrations, not exact specimens, photogrammetry, field footage or direct evidence for a traveled route. The final rift separates dry movement and water entry; the two impressions stay attached to their own bed models.
- **Sound:** original dry VO retained digitally before AAC; music has longer bridges and reduced level under contact/rock action. Tactile material attacks share the picture frame; unmotivated type hits and the Mayo title droplet are gone. Separate foreground, ambience and score management; lower 2.5–5 kHz flowing-water energy during erosion and the valley, a local Sousa bank lap, then dry fracture with delayed ocean. The audio guard verifies exact sample subtraction, decoded alignment, stereo/mono bandwidth and true peak.

## Reproduce

1. Install the project Python stack (`numpy==2.2.6`, `scipy==1.15.3`, `Pillow==11.3.0`, `imageio-ffmpeg==0.6.0`, `pyshp==2.3.1`), `g++` with OpenMP, and `gh` for the pinned asset builder. Run from the repo root with those Python packages on `PYTHONPATH` or in the environment. On two cores use `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` for picture and `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` for sound.
2. Restore the existing Step 3 narration/SRT/timing files to `deliverables/extinct-life-step-3/` (symlinks are fine). Put the archived IC MP4 (checksum in Step 7) in `.cache/monarch-extinct/atlantic-tracks/film/cinema-master/inputs/`. `step-6/assets.py` restores the pinned fields, typefaces and material inputs. `step-6/sound_v2.py` restores the previous stereo separation for comparison.
3. `python3 tools/extinct-life/step-8/audit.py` scans the baseline; `python3 tools/extinct-life/step-8/render.py --group all` streams native CGI plates to encoded 1080p24 shot segments. `--samples` and `--shots` are internal QA and selective rebuilds. Worksets and raw audio never go into Git.
4. `python3 tools/extinct-life/step-8/mix_cinema.py` rebuilds the stereo WAV and its isolated overlay/foreground. `python3 tools/extinct-life/step-8/conform.py --audio-preflight` checks the encoding against all caption windows. `python3 tools/extinct-life/step-8/conform.py` replaces the pending export with the named master only after passing the full decode, exact track/timebase checks and visual and audio probes. Caption and chapter sample payloads are copied from the locked archive, not recreated. `--verify-only` is non-encoding verification.
5. Before public release, a human should check **Sousa** at **03:34–03:39**. The take lock documents speech spelling *Sohza*; the original track is unchanged. No publication or permission change is made by this pipeline.

## Research decisions

- Science follows the [Step 2 evidence ledger](../step-2/ATLANTIC-TRACKS-EVIDENCE.md): [Jacobs et al.](https://igeo.ufrj.br/inc/isc/3/03_139_Jacobs%20etal.pdf), [Menkem et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jop.2026.100416), and [Carvalho et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cretres.2013.04.004). The 125 Ma coastlines and PaleoDEMs are adapted under CC BY 4.0; credits are embedded and listed in the handoff. No third-party field imagery or scientific figures are reused as footage.
- The [Tectonic Globe movement reference](https://github.com/Design-in-Product/globe/blob/72b68a3e18c8c5e4373c33a34b6afde7894bcbf5/tectonic_globe_v6.mp4) was examined for limb readability and kept as a reference only. The in-film globe samples our locked fields. The response implements a custom compressive shoulder (compare [Khronos's discussion of neutral highlight compression](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/ToneMapping/tree/main/PBR_Neutral)); water uses a Fresnel term, path-length approximation and directional normal groups (compare [Filament](https://github.com/google/filament) and [WaterCuda](https://github.com/SamG-Coder/WaterCuda)). The code and the scene geometry in this revision are not imported from those projects.
- The [Step 7 audio study](../step-7/globe-and-sound-review.md) distinguished running-water masking from transient splash. The checked mix reduces those beds locally rather than processing the narration or turning all effects down. Source recordings with exact checksums and licensing come from the [Step 6 sound lock](../step-6/sound-v2-sources.json). FFmpeg EBU R128, lossless pre-AAC sample subtraction, caption-window correlation and phone-band decoding are separate checks.

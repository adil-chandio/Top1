This Ant Jumps by Biting Sand

DESCRIPTION — paste the entire block below
A trap-jaw ant snaps its jaws in midair. No lift. The same snap against the sand launches it.

One jump clears the antlion pit. Another hits the wall and falls back in. In the lab, researchers glued the tips of some ants' jaws shut. Could they still escape without the snap?

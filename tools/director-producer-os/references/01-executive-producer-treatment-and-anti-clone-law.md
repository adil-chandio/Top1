# 01 — Creative Producer Treatment, Epistemic Ledger & Anti-Clone World Selection Law

## 1. Why Videos Start Looking Identical (Forensic Root-Cause Diagnosis)
When an AI agent or rushed production team receives a new topic without an explicit **Executive Producer Treatment**, it unconsciously falls back to its safest cached setup:
- **Same Void Room:** Dark `#09121A` obsidian room with faint grid floor.
- **Same Centerpiece Prop:** Specimen sitting on a rotating circular pedestal or optical lab table.
- **Same Two Colors:** Teal/cyan (`#3AD4BE`) + gold/amber (`#F7C35F`) rim lights.
- **Same HUD:** Top-left monospace telemetry pill + bottom metric strip.
- **Same Cut Structure:** `Exterior spin -> X-ray wireframe cutaway -> Glow climax`.

Even when the 3D geometry is pristine, **repeating those 5 choices across Thermal Glass, Fossils, Biology, and Engineering turns the viewer blind within 0.8 seconds** ("I have already seen this template").

## 2. The Top1 Anti-Clone Mandate (Hard Law)
Every video produced in this repository MUST earn its own **World DNA** from its subject matter. Before any script timing, voice lock, or Three.js file is created, the Producer locks **7 orthogonal creative decisions**:

| Dimension | Question Answered | Anti-Clone Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1. World Archetype (`W01–W24`)** | *Where does this story physically live?* | Never repeat `archetype_id` back-to-back studio-wide or on the same channel. |
| **2. Staging Topology** | *How is the physical environment arranged?* | After `<=2` sculptural/pedestal stages, MUST move into a real world (`in_situ_natural_biome`, `lived_in_human_interior`, `field_expedition_site`, `industrial_test_bay`, `microscopic_or_cosmic_diegetic`, `split_reality_dual_timeline`). |
| **3. Primary Prop Anchor** | *What grounds the object in reality?* | Never repeat `turntable_pedestal` / `optical_breadboard` consecutively (`Jaccard < 0.50`). |
| **4. Palette & Lighting Ratio** | *What Kelvin sources & contrast ratio (`1.5:1` vs `12:1`) define the mood?* | Block `obsidian + teal/cyan + amber` if used in `>=2` of the last 3 episodes. |
| **5. Opening Hook Grammar** | *How do the first `0.0–2.0s` arrest the eye?* | Rotate across `IMPOSSIBLE_SCALE_JUXTAPOSITION`, `MID_ACTION_CONSEQUENCE_FIRST`, `FAMILIAR_OBJECT_STRANGE_BEHAVIOR`, `FORENSIC_CRIME_SCENE_RESULT`, `SILENT_ANOMALY_CLOSEUP`, `SPLIT_COMPARISON_PARADOX`, `HUMAN_SCALE_WITNESS_POV`, `TIME_FROZEN_CATASTROPHE`. |
| **6. Sequence Grammar** | *How does shot order build comprehension?* | Rotate across 7 distinct editorial sequence patterns instead of repeating `pedestal -> cutaway`. |
| **7. HUD Philosophy (`H0–H5`)** | *Does text belong on screen at all — and if so, in what graphic language?* | Choose deliberately among `H0_PURE_CINEMA` (zero HUD!), `H1_DOCUMENTARY_LOWER_THIRD`, `H2_DIEGETIC_IN_WORLD`, `H3_FORENSIC_BLUEPRINT`, `H4_HIGH_CONTRAST_SWISS_GRID`, `H5_EDITORIAL_CHAPTER_CARDS`. |

Overall weighted divergence against the previous studio production MUST score **`>= 65.0%`** in `anti_clone_validator.py`.

## 3. Topic-DNA -> World Archetype Decision Matrix
Never pick a style randomly. Derive the world from **where the phenomenon has maximum physical Stakes and Scale**:

1. **Everyday Household Physics / Kitchen / Desi Courtyard / Street Failure**
   - *Best Worlds:* `W01_SUNLIT_DOMESTIC_MORNING` (`5400K` window sun, ceramic/wood/linen), `W22_DESI_ROOFTOP_COURTYARD_DUSK` (charpai, terracotta, warm dusk bulb + twilight sky), `W09_MONSOON_ASPHALT_NIGHT_STREET`, `W13_TERRACOTTA_CARPENTER_WORKSHOP`.
   - *Why:* A kitchen glass shattering or pressure cooker venting hits `10x` harder on a real sunlit kitchen counter with morning steam than on a sci-fi dark pedestal!
2. **Living Wildlife / Insect / Plant / Marine Behavior**
   - *Best Worlds:* `W05_RAINFOREST_CANOPY_UNDERSTORY`, `W16_GOLDEN_HOUR_SAVANNA_GRASSLAND`, `W03_ABYSSAL_HYDROTHERMAL_DARK`, `W21_TIDAL_COASTAL_BASALT_WAVE_TANK`, `W11_CELLULAR_INNER_CATHEDRAL`.
   - *HUD:* Prefer `H0_PURE_CINEMA` or `H1_DOCUMENTARY_LOWER_THIRD` (BBC Earth / Lubezki discipline — let organic texture breathe without sci-fi brackets).
3. **Deep-Time Paleontology / Geology / Mass Extinctions**
   - *Best Worlds:* `W02_SCORCHED_FIELD_EXPEDITION`, `W07_CRYOGENIC_GLACIAL_WHITEOUT`, `W14_VOLCANIC_BASALT_EXTINCTION`, `W08_CANDLELIT_NATURALIST_ARCHIVE`, `W17_SUBTERRANEAN_KARST_LIMESTONE_CAVE`, `W24_DUAL_STRAND_OPPENHEIMER_CONTRAST` (modern field trench cross-cut with living deep-time biome).
   - *HUD:* `H5_EDITORIAL_CHAPTER_CARDS` or `H1_DOCUMENTARY_LOWER_THIRD` or `H2_DIEGETIC_IN_WORLD` (field notebook / stratigraphic ruler).
4. **Heavy Metallurgy / Engines / Structural Failure / Aerospace**
   - *Best Worlds:* `W04_BRUTALIST_FOUNDRY_HIGH_HEAT`, `W15_AERODYNAMIC_WIND_TUNNEL_BAY`, `W23_HIGH_VOLTAGE_FARADAY_DARKROOM`, `W12_HARD_VACUUM_ORBITAL_SUNLIGHT`.
5. **Clean Precision / Medical / Semiconductor / Pure Concept Comparison**
   - *Best Worlds:* `W06_HIGH_KEY_SCULPTURAL_ATELIER` (bright white `#F5F7FA` softbox gallery!), `W10_SURGICAL_OPERATING_THEATER`, `W18_ARCHITECTURAL_BLUEPRINT_CUTAWAY`, `W19_SYNCHROTRON_TOMOGRAPHY_CHAMBER`.

## 4. The Epistemic Honesty Ledger (`EV0 / EV1 / EV2`)
Adopted and sharpened from open-source documentary governance (`universal-ai-film-director` + `monarch-studio-os`):
Every `producer_brief.epistemic_ledger` separates every claim BEFORE visual design begins:
- **`observed_evidence` (`EV0`):** Direct measurements, specimen IDs, physical constants, peer-reviewed empirical numbers (e.g., *"Lead-bismuth-tin safety plug melts at ~240°C"*). Shown with photographic realism.
- **`scientific_inferences` (`EV1`):** Best-supported causal reconstruction (e.g., *"Estivation sheltered the pair from peak surface heat pulses"*). Spoken with calibrated verbs (*"points to"*, *"indicates"*), never false certainty.
- **`illustrative_abstractions` (`EV2`):** False-color stress gradients, velocity streamlines, cross-section planes, magnified cellular speedups. Must look unmistakably analytical/explanatory where simplified so the audience is never deceived.

# Source and use notes for the Huayuan next review

- **Comparative fossil photograph:** Anomalocaris canadensis grasping claw, Burgess Shale, British Columbia, Canada, specimen YPM 35138. Photo by James St. John, CC BY 2.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anomalocaris_canadensis_grasping_claw,_Burgess_Shale.jpg ; license https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/ . The image is a lower-resolution version, displayed on angled surfaces and adjusted in contrast/tone. It is a comparative fossil, NOT a Huayuan specimen and does not illustrate all the narrated soft-tissue features. Name and locality appear on screen.
- Generic reconstruction models: Anomalocaris by Skache, Opabinia by billionlioe, Hallucigenia by huykhoi2407; all CC BY 4.0. Original URLs, derivative details, SHA-256 hashes and rock asset credits: `../notes/SOURCE-AND-RECOVERY.md`. CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The models are modified in material, scale, rig timing and placement; they are not identified Huayuan species.
- Science reference: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10030-0 . The proposed deep-water refuge is a hypothesis, not established causality. Crisis and sediment depictions are illustrative, time-compressed, and not records of a locally observed event.
- Voice: the recovered approved story MP4 audio is an existing stereo mix; it contains narration plus old music and effects. The verified dry narration stem is not available. Recovered AAC stream decoded PCM SHA-256 `6bb74d9c8645b6e4ea1438ade1a7738d7d69865b013b1b5c2c581f2e30ab9348`; it will be stream-copied unchanged, no stretch. No independent new audio mix is claimed.

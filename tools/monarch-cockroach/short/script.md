## Beat-by-Beat Script, Camera & Contact Directives

> Spoken lines appear in blockquotes (identical to [`narration.txt`](narration.txt)). `PICTURE ONLY` and `SUPER` notes are visual/editorial instructions, never spoken.

### Beat 01 · `0:00.0–0:06.2` (`f0–f186`) · THE TWO-PENNY SLIT
> Standing up, this cockroach is half an inch tall. Watch it vanish under two stacked pennies.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Cold open at cockroach eye height (`f0`): a freestanding `12.52 mm` *Periplaneta americana* sprints across a clean tabletop toward a clear acrylic gate set to `3.2 mm` beside two stacked US pennies (`~3.04 mm`). At `f66`, antennae sweep through the slit first; at `f90`, the head pitches downward and rams into the opening; at `f150`, the pronotum rolls slightly side-to-side to enter. Keep pennies, gate, and floor in one continuous spatial frame so the `4:1` height drop is undeniable without exaggeration.
- **SUPER (`f12–f120`, upper-left safe zone):** `Illustrative reconstruction · 12.5 mm standing → 3.2 mm slit`

### Beat 02 · `0:06.2–0:13.4` (`f186–f402`) · RIGID PLATES, SOFT HINGES
> Its shell isn't bending like soft rubber. Stiff armor plates slide together as the soft joints between them fold in half.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Side sagittal cutaway under the `3.2 mm` acrylic ceiling. Ghost the outer wing covers (`tegmina`) at 30% opacity so the dorsal sclerotized tergites read as rigid amber plates (`constant chord length`, zero rubbery bending). Highlight the soft **arthrodial membranes** in subtle teal as they fold (`47 ± 3%` abdominal compression at `3.2 mm`), shingling each anterior plate cleanly over the next with zero mesh interpenetration (`f294` zoom inset).
- **SUPER (`f258–f390`):** `Rigid chitin plates · Soft arthrodial joints (~47% compression)`

### Beat 03 · `0:13.4–0:21.4` (`f402–f642`) · CLAWS OFF, SHIN SPINES ON
> Pressed that flat, its legs splay wide and its claws lose their angle. Sharp spines along its shins push the floor instead.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Cut to front transverse cutaway (`f402`) matching `12.5 mm` upright claw stance (`sprawl ~55°`, tarsal claws planted at `z = 0 mm`) against `4.0 mm` confinement (`sprawl ~80°`, `f438`). Hold macro close-up on the right hind leg (`f474–f642`): show the distal `tarsus` and terminal claws splayed outward and lifted `> 0.4 mm` clear of normal grip (`f504`), while three angled sensory spines on the `tibia` plant cleanly on the substrate (`z = 0 mm`, `f582`) and drive the body forward. No foot sliding during the stick phase.
- **SUPER (`f510–f636`):** `Claws disengaged · Tibial spines take the load`

### Beat 04 · `0:21.4–0:27.4` (`f642–f822`) · THE SYNCHRONOUS SHOVE
> Its normal running rhythm breaks down, and both middle legs shove together to drive the body forward.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Overhead transparent-ceiling view at `4.0 mm` (`f642`). Briefly ghost the `6 mm` alternating tripod markers (`L1–R2–L3` / `R1–L2–R3`), then show `4.0 mm` confinement breaking that tripod (`f672`): both mesothoracic (middle) legs fire in synchrony (`f756`) like paired oars pushing against floor friction while dorsal plates skim the acrylic ceiling.

### Beat 05 · `0:27.4–0:34.2` (`f822–f1026`) · FRICTION TRAP & THE 3.2 mm BOTTLENECK
> Even then, rough ceilings slow it to a slipping zigzag. At three millimeters, most turn back—and nearly one in ten gets wedged.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Split/sequential limit proof:
  1. `f822–f894`: Swap smooth ceiling for rough ceiling texture — dorsal drag arrows oppose motion, tibial spines cross into slip phase, speed drops (`58 cm/s → 14.5 cm/s` and slower under high ceiling drag), and the trajectory wobbles into a tortuous zigzag.
  2. `f894–f1026`: Return to the `3.2 mm` gate for the honest lab outcome breakdown (`315` trials): `72%` pass at `6.1 mm` vs only `17%` at `3.2 mm`; show one cockroach probing with antennae and backing out (dominant failure mode), and another briefly wedged at the thickest thoracic segment (`9%` stuck, `f984`) — no crushing or gore.
- **SUPER (`f894–f1020`):** `315 lab trials · 17% pass at 3.2 mm · 9% wedged at thorax`

### Beat 06 · `0:34.2–0:38.0` (`f1026–f1140`) · REBOUND & LOOP SEAM
> Armor doesn't make it unstoppable. Hinges give it a way out.

- **PICTURE ONLY:** Follow the successful `17%` runner clearing the `3.2 mm` gate (`f1050`): the moment the abdomen exits, viscoelastic arthrodial hinges spring the body back up to `12.52 mm` freestanding height (`60%` resilience at low rate, zero injury), tarsal claws re-engage in full alternating tripod sprint, matching the exact camera height, lighting, and tabletop geography of `f0` at `f1139` for a seamless Shorts loop.

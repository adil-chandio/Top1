YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION
Flip on a light, and an American cockroach (Periplaneta americana) half an inch tall can bolt straight under a slot barely as thick as two stacked pennies (3.2 mm) in under a second—and keep moving inside a 4 mm space where its feet can no longer stand underneath its body.

Following 500 fps biomechanical tests step by step, this film breaks down the five-move doorway squeeze, how stiff exoskeleton plates overlap like roof tiles on folding arthrodial hinges, why speed holds steady down to half standing height before crashing two millimeters lower, how stiff shin spines dig in like crampons, why the coarsest floor actually slows the crawl, and why more than four out of five attempts still fail at 3.2 mm.

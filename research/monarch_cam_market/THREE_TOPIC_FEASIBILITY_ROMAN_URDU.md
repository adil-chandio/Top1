# Monarch Cam — 3-topic evidence aur visual-feasibility gate

## Decision

**Pehla conditional candidate: humpback whales orca hunts mein dakhal kyun deti hain?** Isko topic-development ke liye select karein, production ke liye abhi nahi. Scientific premise supported hai; actual interaction footage aur unique moving visuals ki rights-clearance unresolved hai. Teeno candidates ka final production gate abhi HOLD hai. Kisi asset ko cleared, purchased ya commissioned nahi kaha ja raha.

Yeh factual feasibility pack hai, script, storyboard ya rendered look-dev nahi. Neeche editorial judgments hain; fake numeric scores, cost quotes ya guaranteed views nahi.

## A. Humpback–orca interference

**Viewer question:** ek humpback doosri species par ho rahe attack mein kyun approach/interfere karegi?

**Supported:** Pitman et al. ka study 115 interactions review karta hai. Mammal-eating killer whales ke attacks ke saath mobbing/interference documented hai; kuch prey escape outcomes report hue. **Uncertain:** har interaction ka motive; deliberate moral rescue universal conclusion nahi. [Primary repository](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/54369); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/mms.12343).

**Banned claims:** `humpbacks hamesha jeet ti hain`, `orcas kisi humpback ko nahi maar sakti`, `har humpback jaan boojh kar har animal ko rescue karti hai`, `sirf ek animal orcas ko dara sakta hai`. Mammal-eating aur fish-eating ecotypes ko interchangeable na dikhayein.

**Distinct value:** competitor ke fear framing ki jagah observed behaviour, competing explanations aur uncertainty samjhana. Yeh scientific editorial choice hai, equal viral performance ki guarantee nahi.

## B. Orcas aur white sharks

**Viewer question:** ek specific shark carcass par DNA se predator kaise identify hua, aur liver-selective feeding ke liye kya evidence hai?

**Supported:** 2025 study ka abstract October 2023 Australian carcass, major wound mein killer-whale DNA aur other bites mein sevengill shark DNA report karta hai. Liver-selective consumption likely inference hai; har orca population ka universal routine nahi. [PubMed primary-study record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39872902/).

**Banned claims:** har great white ko har orca hunt karti hai; har encounter same technique use karta hai; ek local observation global shark disappearance explain karta hai. Tonic immobility ko is specific Australian event ka directly witnessed fact na banayein.

**Visual route:** original sampling-evidence animation, clean non-graphic anatomical diagram, independently created location map. PubMed figure caption multiple photographer credits deta hai; abstract availability individual image clearance nahi. Raw wound/carcass photos is pack mein download/use nahi kiye. Observed encounter ki film ko Australian DNA case ke actual footage ki tarah label nahi karenge.

**Trade-off:** strong scientific detective question, lekin graphic-focus ad risk aur same ocean-predator topic repetition. Verified rights, anatomical accuracy aur production capability ke baghair backup only.

## C. Vampire squid: naam scary, feeding unexpected

**Viewer question:** vampire squid apne filaments aur mucus se sinking organic material kaise collect karta hai?

**Supported:** Hoving & Robison 2012 research aur MBARI article marine-snow collection ko describe karte hain. Marine snow mein dead organic particles, faecal pellets aur mucus ho sakte hain; isko `blood hunting` kehna ghalat. Tiny living hitchhiking crustaceans ki caveat ki wajah se `kabhi koi living organism nahi khata` absolute claim avoid karein. [MBARI research explanation](https://www.mbari.org/news/mbari-researchers-discover-what-vampire-squids-eat-its-not-what-you-think/); DOI 10.1098/rspb.2012.1357.

**Available evidence visuals:** MBARI page par filament aur feeding photos explicitly copyrighted hain. Ye actual scientific visual leads hain, free stock nahi. Proposed original filament/particle-flow animation ko anatomical review chahiye, aur usko actual microscopy footage nahi kahenge.

**Hard rights blocker:** [MBARI legal terms](https://www.mbari.org/legal/) default non-commercial classroom use tak restricted hain. [Media request](https://www.mbari.org/about/for-media/media-request/) route says stock requests supported nahi, fees possible, photo ke liye minimum two weeks aur video ke liye four weeks advised. [VARS terms](https://www.mbari.org/technology/video-annotation-and-reference-system-vars/vars-data-use-policy/) bhi YouTube use ke liye separate written permission require karte hain. Monetized documentary eligibility assume nahi kar rahe; koi request send nahi hui aur koi fee approve nahi.

**Decision:** high explanatory interest, lekin first low-friction production candidate nahi. Primary research availability ko production-asset availability se confuse na karein.

## Rights policy jo teeno par apply hoti hai

[NOAA Fisheries policy](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/about-us/website-policies-and-disclaimers) NOAA-created photos aur third-party credited photos ko distinguish karti hai. [NOAA FAQ](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/about/faq.html) specifically videos ko case-by-case rakhta hai kyunki third-party footage ho sakti hai. Government domain ya downloadable file apne aap blanket commercial clearance nahi. Credit, permit notices, creator, modification restrictions aur no-endorsement requirement item level par ledger mein record honge. Yeh clearance checklist hai, legal guarantee nahi.

## Agla concrete gate — topic A

1. Specific interaction-motion asset ki item-level source/owner/licence ya documented original-animation alternative establish karein; no competitor clip downloads.
2. Har indispensable claim ke saath primary citation aur uncertainty note freeze karein.
3. Actual moving-shot feasibility ledger banayein: unique action, visual owner, permission status, accuracy risk. Generic scenery se missing behaviour hide nahi karna.
4. Gate pass ho tab title-thumbnail/story-outline approval pack. Abhi creative approval implied nahi.
5. User ke HAAN ke baad look-dev stills; phir HAAN ke baad 5-second motion proof; phir HAAN ke baad full production. Koi gate skip nahi.

**Bottom line:** pehla topic choose ho gaya as a conditional research lead, magar footage availability pretend karke production start nahi hui. Agar core visual gate fail hota hai to budget consume karne se pehle topic badlega.

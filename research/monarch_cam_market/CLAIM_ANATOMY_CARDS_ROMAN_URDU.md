## Anatomy reference sheet (textual, not approved model sheet)

**Humpback:** mostly dark body; variable white on pectorals, underside and flukes; long pectoral fins; serrated trailing edge and pointed fluke tips. Baleen, not toothed mouth. Individuals/hemispheres vary: no universal identical white belly or cloned fluke markings. Source S2. NOAA illustration/photo is reference, not automatic copied texture/mesh licence.

**Orca:** black dorsal surface, white underside and eye patches, saddle patch behind dorsal fin; markings vary by population and individual; adult males proportionally larger dorsal fins/flippers/flukes and girth. Source S3. Not all animals in a group should get identical giant adult-male dorsal fin.

**Scale:** NOAA lists species upper bounds (humpback up to ~60ft/40 tons; orca up to ~32ft/11 tons). These are upper bounds, not selected individuals, averages or a valid exact ratio. Draft needs no mass-number showdown. Units as stated on source; no unmarked metric conversion. Specific scale drawing remains pending.

**Motion production checks:** coherent articulated swimming, fluke propulsion instead of fish-like lateral tail whipping, no fin-body interpenetration, no whale gills, no baleen-whale teeth, no false contact, no levitating/surface-plane errors. These are proposed QC rules, not a motion test passed. Detailed kinematics/frequency/turning performance not verified in this pass.

**Sound:** illustrative cue only; don't call invented audio an actual call or accurate animal hearing simulation. No sonic blast/weapon depiction.

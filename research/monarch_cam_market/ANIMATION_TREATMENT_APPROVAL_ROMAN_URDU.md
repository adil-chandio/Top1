## Recommended editorial treatment

**Core question:** Humpback whales kabhi mammal-eating orcas ke attacks mein interfere kyun karti hain, jab prey doosri species bhi ho sakti hai?

**Viewer promise:** observed behaviour, plausible explanations aur scientific uncertainty ko compelling moving explanation mein separate karke samjhayenge. Orcas ko villains aur humpbacks ko universally invincible heroes nahi banayenge.

**Format:** original cinematic scientific animation. Photoreal documentary footage hone ka claim nahi. Reconstructed behaviours par `Illustrative reconstruction` label; kisi specific encounter ki exact choreography bina event evidence reproduce nahi karni. Future narration/on-screen language ko separately lock karna hai; is pack ki English wording packaging candidates hain, approved spoken script nahi.

**Length:** evidence aur unique visual coverage se decide hogi. Abhi arbitrary 8–10 minutes fill karne ka target nahi. Topic ko stretch ya recycled shots se pad nahi karenge.

## Story spine — narration/script nahi

### 1. Observable puzzle

Ek clearly labelled illustrative interruption se spatial question establish ho: approach karne wala animal khud potential predation risk face karta hai, phir bhi hunt ke paas kyun aata hai? Opening ko invented encounter date/location/eyewitness dialogue nahi dena. Promise exactly intervention explain karne ka ho, `secret orca killer` reveal ka nahi.

**Visual need:** animal locomotion, body volume, water displacement aur readable blocking. Slide/pan-zoom acceptable nahi.

### 2. Orca ek single feeding stereotype nahi

Mammal-eating aur fish-eating forms ka relevant distinction. Is study ka sample predominantly mammal-eating encounters hai; saare orcas ko mammal hunters na dikhayein. Evidence-led markings/anatomy references chahiye; arbitrary skin patterns nahi.

**Payoff:** predator label se zyada specific hunting ecology matter karti hai.

### 3. Scientists ne actually kya review kiya

115 interaction accounts ka high-level context; initiator known subset n=72 aur classification subset n=108 ko alag clearly show karna. 57% ya 95% ko global chance/experiment result nahi. Percentages ki parade nahi—sirf essential statistic jo question clarify kare.

**Visual need:** independently designed animated evidence grouping; paper ka figure/layout trace nahi. Research accounts ki recording coverage aur sampling bias acknowledge karna.

### 4. Interference practically kya hota hai

Approach, harassment/mobbing aur appendage displays ko paper-supported descriptions se distinguish karna. Animal-to-animal direct impact, specific escape outcome ya striking action sirf evidence ho to depict; cinematic contact invent nahi.

**Payoff:** large appendages aur close approach se hunt disrupt ho sakti hai; iska matlab har hunt stop hona nahi.

### 5. Doosri species ka puzzle

Review mein non-humpback prey bhi included. Observed event outcome aur explanation ko separate show: initial response to attack sounds ek proposed explanation hai; calf defence aur other-species interference ko same proven motive nahi. Underwater audio ko actual incident recording nahi label karna; sound propagation simulation ho to illustrative label.

**Payoff:** behaviour observed hai; intention ki complete access nahi.

### 6. Honest resolution

Mobbing sometimes prey escape se associated hai; altruism even if unintentional rule out nahi hua. Conclusion neither `just selfish` nor `proven moral superheroes`. Opening ka answer: documented interference aur plausible mechanisms samajh aaye, universal motive abhi unresolved.

**End value:** viewer ko scientific observation aur attractive story ke beech difference samajh aaye; recycled teaser ending ya exaggerated future promise nahi.

## Animation quality ka operational definition

`Tabhai level` ko hype ki jagah inspectable checks mein convert karenge:

- Accurate species silhouettes, flipper proportions, fluke orientation and scale; orcas fish-style side-to-side tail swimming nahi karengi.
- Genuine articulated movement aur coherent trajectory; still-image camera push ko animation nahi kahenge.
- Animal mass, acceleration, turns aur camera movement consistent. Rubber-body bends, fin intersections, sliding animals aur cloned background formations fail.
- Underwater light/visibility plausible; shot dark karke anatomy/motion errors hide nahi karna.
- Different shots different informational jobs karenge. Same asset ka reused camera angle automatically unique visual nahi.
- No glowing monster eyes, baleen-whale teeth, gratuitous wounds, invented attack choreography or giant scale without reference.
- Labels readable, reconstructions disclosed; diagrams aur cinematic reconstruction visually distinguishable.
- Original meshes/rigs/textures/music ya verified licences; GitHub repository ka code licence bundled model/texture rights ka automatic proof nahi.

**Capability warning:** premium biological animation deliverable abhi proven nahi. Renderer/tool ka naam quality proof nahi. Approval ke liye actual stills aur later motion dikhani hogi, verbal promise nahi.

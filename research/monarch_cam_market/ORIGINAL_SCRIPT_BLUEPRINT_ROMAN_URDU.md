# Monarch Cam — original script research blueprint v1

## Scope

Yeh evidence-led story blueprint hai, spoken narration nahi. Script-forensic study ke baad previous treatment ko refine karta hai. User ne animation-led route accept kiya; P0 stills, P1 motion aur full film approvals abhi separate hain. Koi generated asset, audio, render ya upload is document ke saath nahi.

**Working question:** Humpback whales kabhi orca hunts mein dakhal kyun deti hain?

**Working title:** Why Humpback Whales Interrupt Orca Hunts

**Promise contract:** viewer ko intervention ka observable behaviour, possible mechanism aur motive ke evidence limits samajh aayen. Hum universal fear, invincibility ya proved moral rescue promise nahi karte.

**Distinct structure:** intervention ko ek evidence problem ki tarah unpack karna; na three-animal countdown, na shark-rival elimination, na competitor ke anecdotes/jokes ki same chain. Real emotional tension: ek protective-looking action ko dekh kar hum kitna samajh sakte hain, aur kitna assume kar dete hain?

## Evidence boundary

Primary source S1: Pitman et al., Marine Mammal Science 33(1), DOI 10.1111/mms.12343. Primary PDF: https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/54369/noaa_54369_DS1.pdf . Review of 115 accounts, not controlled experiment or census. Existing exact-denominator notes: `VISUAL_GATE_ROMAN_URDU.md`.

Blueprint ke mechanisms/hypotheses S1 ki reading se hain. Before final narration, detailed physical scene behaviour ko exact passage/event se lock karna zaroori. Is pack mein kisi identified encounter ki exact route, speed, distance, dialogue ya interior emotion invent nahi.

## Story ka governing principle

**Observation → physical possibility → limited evidence → possible explanation.** Audience ko har answer par agla valid question mile. Main answer ko end tak lock karke suspense nahi banayenge. Early body mein partial explanation denge; ending uncertainty ko precise banayegi, answer evade nahi karegi.

## Beat 1 — Intervention ko pehle dekhna, intention baad mein

**Central question:** yeh approach kya change kar sakti hai?

**Content job:** clearly labelled schematic reconstruction: hunting orcas aur potential prey ki relative position mein humpback approach. No bite/contact/rescue outcome depicted until documented event or supported action is chosen. Humpback ka presence scene ko badalta dikh sakta hai, lekin exact successful escape fictitiously stage nahi karna.

**Opening architecture, not dialogue:** ongoing spatial problem → unexpected approach → question. No creator introduction, no whale-size trivia dump, no claims ke saara ocean darta hai. First meaningful information is the behaviour itself.

**Early payoff:** interference observed behaviour hai; `rescue` uska possible interpretation hai, direct motive measurement nahi. Yeh distinction early milna chahiye, disclaimer sirf ending mein nahi.

**Next question:** hunt ke paas aana useful kaise ho sakta hai?

**Unique motion need:** independent humpback trajectory, coherent orca movement, visible before/after spatial relationship. Model intersections, repeated loops ya camera-only still fail.

**Evidence:** S1 mobbing/interference descriptions. **Pending:** exact supported action/reference selection. **Reject if:** reconstruction ko 2009/2012/2022 ka authentic filmed event imply kare.

## Beat 2 — Physical interference, superhero combat nahi

**Central question:** teeth ke baghair animal hunt ko disrupt kaise kar sakta hai?

**Content job:** species silhouette aur appendages ko functional geometry se explain. Approach, displays, proximity aur hunt interruption ko separate ideas rakhein. Paper ki mobbing descriptions aur actual confirmed contact ko conflate nahi karna.

**Payoff:** hunt affect karne ke liye predator ko kill karna zaroori nahi; action ki cost/coordination alter ho sakti hai. Yeh explanatory inference hai, har event ka measured energy budget nahi.

**Next question:** agar adults ko approach karna risky hai, orcas humpbacks ke paas aati kyun hain?

**Unique motion need:** articulated flipper/fluke motion with safe separation; changing usable approach space visually explain, not invented collision force. Anatomical scale references pending; arbitrary spectacular impact reject.

**Evidence:** S1 behaviour/defence discussion. **Do not claim:** flipper definitely breaks an orca's bones, every approach forces retreat, humpbacks harmlessly win every encounter.

## Beat 3 — Adult aur calf ek jaisa defensive problem nahi

**Central question:** vulnerable calves ke context se intervention ka kya relation banta hai?

**Content job:** adult-vs-calf vulnerability aur defensive positioning explain; mammal-eating forms ko all orcas se distinguish. Graphic drowning/killing reenactment necessary nahi. Positioning-based explanation preferred.

**Payoff:** calf defence intervention ke liye plausible evolutionary context deta hai. Ab adult strength aur predation risk apparent contradiction nahi.

**Evidence detail:** S1 ke mammal-eating-orca-approach subset n=27 mein 85% attacks aur only-calf targets reported. Narration mein exact percentage zaroori nahi; use hua to denominator on-screen aur collection limits clear.

**Next question:** kya intervention sirf apne calves ke liye hoti hai?

**Unique motion need:** protective repositioning and change of viewing scale. No animal transformed into another or misleading speed/size ratio. Behaviour details need passage-specific reference.

**Reject if:** all orcas mammal hunters, rake mark proves exact age, or escort motives declared established fact.

## Beat 4 — Dataset us simple explanation ko complicate karta hai

**Central question:** doosri species prey ho to explanation kaise badalti hai?

**Content job:** review ke accounts ka limited evidence view: identified prey mein non-humpback species bhi documented. Pehle spatial scenes ke baad data ka purpose clear hai: hamein intention kyun reconsider karna pad raha hai.

**Payoff:** parental defence alone har observed case ko fully describe nahi karti. Iska matlab altruism proved nahi; sirf harder question established.

**Evidence detail:** S1 abstract gives 11% humpback prey / 89% other species in specified attacking-interaction context. `89% rescues succeeded` kabhi nahi. Exact count/denominator table se lock kiye baghair dramatic rescue counter create nahi karna.

**Next question:** humpback ko approach karne se pehle pata hota hai kis par attack ho raha hai?

**Unique motion need:** original animated grouping where each category label means documented account context; not fabricated reconstructed 115 incident clips. Science-review layer cinematic reconstruction se visually different.

**Reject if:** unrelated stock animal movement pretends to be those accounts; bars imply random global sample.

## Beat 5 — Trigger aur benefit ek cheez nahi

**Central question:** approaching behaviour initiate kaise ho sakta hai, aur kaun benefit pa sakta hai?

**Content job:** sound-response hypothesis ko observation se distinguish. Attack vocalizations par response proposed hai; receiver ko initially prey identity maloom na ho sakti. Calf-defence-related explanation aur other-species beneficiary mutually exclusive slogans nahi.

**Payoff:** animal ko har species identify karke moral choice karna necessary nahi for another species to benefit. Magar deliberate help ko conclusively disprove bhi nahi karte.

**Next question:** phir hum responsibly kis conclusion tak ja sakte hain?

**Unique motion need:** source-labelled schematic sound cue with clearly marked unknowns. Sound direction/range exact data ke baghair measured radius nahi. Sound effect ko actual recording ya humpback ke internal experience ka proof nahi.

**Evidence:** S1 proposed vocalization response and discussion of communal defence / possible interspecific altruism. **Reject if:** `revenge memory`, `they hate orcas`, kin relationship ya conscious motive presented as proven.

## Beat 6 — Outcome ko motive se alag rakhna

**Central question:** successful interruption, unsuccessful interruption aur motive ke beech kya farq hai?

**Content job:** S1 ka qualified result: mobbing sometimes prey escape allow karta hai. Intervention sometimes beneficial ho sakti hai; all cases successful nahi. Agar specific observed case choose hota hai to outcome usi case ke saath reveal—contradictory detail minutes baad hide nahi.

**Payoff:** viewer ko teen distinct questions ka model milta hai: kya kiya? outcome kya hua? kyun kiya? Pehle do par evidence stronger ho sakta hai without complete third answer.

**Next question / closure bridge:** opening reconstruction mein kaunsa hissa observed behaviour se supported tha aur kaunsa illustrative tha?

**Unique motion need:** original opening spatial model return with evidence annotations and genuinely changed animal state/action. Same still/camera shot replay runtime fill nahi. Is callback ka job knowledge integration hai.

**Reject if:** invented success/failure-rate chart, absolute orca cowardice, complete motive certainty.

## Beat 7 — Earned ending

**Main answer:** documented humpback interference hunting encounters ko disrupt kar sakti hai. Physical defence aur proposed response mechanisms partial explanation dete hain; other-species cases motive ko open scientific question banate hain.

**What changes for viewer:** predator hierarchy ek simple fight leaderboard nahi; behaviour aur context matter karte hain. Uncertainty ka matlab `scientists know nothing` nahi—observation aur explanation ke evidence levels different hain.

**Closure:** opening ka unresolved movement ab understandable hai. Koi unrelated humans-as-real-monster twist, compulsory conservation moral, sequel-gated answer ya generic inspirational aphorism nahi. Optional CTA only after substantive conclusion.

**Unique motion need:** integrated explanatory scene ends on intelligible relative positions, not dramatic attack whose outcome evidence doesn't support.

## Causal chain aur retention hypotheses

| Last answer | Agla natural sawal | Viewer ka continue karne ka possible reason |
|---|---|---|
| Approach/interference documented | Useful kaise? | Physical mechanism samajhna |
| Disruption possible without killing | Orcas approach kyun? | Apparent contradiction resolve |
| Calves vulnerable | Sirf apni species? | Explanation ki boundary test |

Yeh proposed attention logic hai, measured retention prediction nahi. Fixed interval par hook repeat, arbitrary question-count score ya dopamine language use nahi.

## Paragraph-level QA before full script

Har proposed paragraph ko annotated pass mile:

- **Job:** naya observation / mechanism / payoff / necessary caveat; agar none, cut.
- **Evidence:** primary source + exact passage/table; missing ho to claim pending, not fact.
- **Scope:** species/population/location/subset correct?
- **Inference:** observed, interpreted, proposed, illustrative mein category?
- **Visual:** unique changing action kya; kya camera-only move hide kar raha?
- **Causality:** `because` ke peeche evidence hai ya seductive story?
- **Loop:** kis question ko answer, agla genuinely kyun necessary?
- **Originality:** competitor ka unusual phrase, story order, same joke or metaphor removed?
- **Runtime:** repetition se length badh rahi hai? Scientific caveat ko remove karke false speed toh nahi?

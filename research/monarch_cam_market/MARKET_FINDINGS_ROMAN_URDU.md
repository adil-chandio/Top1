## Source rights, originality aur production feasibility

- Ocean predator: licensed underwater footage or genuinely original explanatory diagrams chahiye; YouTube competitor clips downloadable hone ka matlab usable rights nahi. Scientific uncertainty aur anthropomorphic claims ka fact-check essential. AI image ko authentic observed wildlife footage ki tarah present na karein.
- Human daily life: original illustration technically feasible, magar archaeology uncertainty, speculative scenes aur labour-heavy unique shots. Yeh Extinct Life se boundary overlap bhi karta hai; is chat me us second channel ka direction automatically change nahi kar rahe.
- Mechanisms: original CAD/diagrams strong differentiation, lekin asset accuracy aur animation workload high. Prior rejected 3D production ko market winner dekh kar validated capability na samjho.
- Geography: original maps/data animation possible; stock maps/borders/data licences aur misleading geopolitical claims check karne hain.
- Humorous commentary: distinctive voice/writing and transformative analysis required; borrowed footage clearance separate. Established host performance ko generic AI voice se equivalent nahi keh sakte.

Official [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en): reused-content review copyright permission se alag hai; repetitive mass-produced material ka bhi risk hai. [Advertiser-friendly guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278?hl=en) ke mutabiq focal graphic suffering risk hai. AI/animation alone policy clearance nahi.

## Monarch Cam ke liye decision

**Abhi hard rebrand ya video production nahi.** Owner ka historical bear winner (~960K), long gaps aur newer weak uploads format-market fit establish nahi karte. Existing subscribers ko guaranteed wildlife audience bhi na assume karein.

Provisional research priority:
1. **Ocean predator misconception / unexpected defence mechanisms**: first wildlife hypothesis to investigate; Apexmorph genuinely recent account aur million-view examples ab verified public metadata me mile. Baseline risk aur sourcing risk dono high.
2. **Animal mechanism / mimicry**: broader scientific editorial identity; daninblue/MinuteEarth comparison useful. Generic `every animal explained` se differentiate karna zaroori.
3. **Prehistoric human everyday problems**: strong cross-niche comparator, automatic Monarch Cam pivot nahi; Extinct Life scope ko alag rakhna hai.
4. Geography / mechanical 3D / generic compilation: demand exists, magar is evidence se Monarch Cam ke liye superior fit prove nahi.

Final creative lock ke pending gates: representative winners + normal/weak uploads ki actual visual review; first 30 seconds, information/payoff timing, original vs licensed assets; science/source audit; realistic unique-shot production feasibility. Private CTR, retention, US audience share, revenue ya organic traffic source public views se nahi pata. Is waqt in par koi claim nahi.

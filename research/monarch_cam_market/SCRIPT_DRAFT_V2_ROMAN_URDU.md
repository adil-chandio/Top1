## 1 — Ulti direction

Orcas shikaar kar rahi hain. Ek humpback unki taraf aa rahi hai.

Yeh whale seals khane nahi aayi. Humpbacks krill aur chhoti machhliyan filter karke khati hain. Phir bhi unhein orcas ke peeche jaate, unka raasta approach karte, aur paani par apne flippers aur dum maarte record kiya gaya hai.

Kuch encounters mein prey ko bach nikalne ka mauqa milta hai.

Lekin har baar nahi. Aur har baar shikaar humpback bhi nahi hota.

Toh aisi whale, jiska khana kuch aur hai, is hunt mein dakhal kyun deti hai?

## 2 — Shikaar rokna, predator ko maarna nahi

Pehle uski movement dekho.

Humpback approach karti hai. Kabhi orcas ka peecha karti hai. Kabhi apne lambe flippers ya dum se paani par display karti hai. Researchers ne aise encounters bhi describe kiye jinmein orcas usse avoid karti ya door hoti nazar aayin.

Is tarah ke behaviour ko mobbing kaha gaya.

Yahan flipper ko orca se takrana zaroori samajhna galti hogi. Surface par slap record hona, direct hit ka proof nahi.

Humpback ko hunt disturb karne ke liye predator ko maarna zaroori nahi. Lekin isse ek doosra sawaal uthta hai: agar adult ke paas itna defence hai, toh orcas humpbacks ke paas aati kyun hain?

Kyunki ek adult aur uska calf ek jaisi position mein nahi hote.

## 3 — Chhoti whale, alag risk

Sab orca populations ek jaisa shikaar nahi karti. Kuch machhliyon par specialise karti hain; doosri marine mammals bhi hunt karti hain.

Humpbacks par attacks ke is review mein calves aur doosre young animals important targets the. Kuch targets ki age uncertain thi.

Ek adult ka defensive behaviour aur ek young whale ki vulnerability—dono saath maujood ho sakte hain. Calf defence isliye ek samajh aane wala context hai.

Magar woh poori kahani nahi.

Records mein seals hain. Sea lions hain. Doosri whales bhi hain.

Agar humpback sirf apne young ko protect kar rahi hai, toh doosri species ke shikaar ke paas kyun pohanchti hai?

## 4 — Records ka uncomfortable hissa

Marine ecologist Robert Pitman aur unke colleagues ne humpbacks aur killer whales ke 115 interactions review kiye.

Yeh 115 rescues nahi the. Reports mein kabhi approach karne wali species clear thi; kabhi observers aaye toh encounter pehle se chal raha tha.

Ek Steller sea lion wale account mein chaar humpbacks maujood thin. Flipper aur dum ke displays record hue. Phir bhi sea lion maara gaya.

Humpback ka aana, prey ka bachna guarantee nahi karta.

Lekin doosre cases mein interference ke saath prey ko escape ka mauqa mila. Isliye na har approach ko successful rescue keh sakte hain, na har intervention ko be-asar.

Ab sawaal jeetne ka nahi. Sawaal yeh hai: humpback ko hunt ki taraf laata kya hai?

## 5 — Pehle cue, phir prey?

Paper mein ek possibility propose ki gayi: humpbacks attacking mammal-eating orcas ki awaazon par respond kar sakti hain, bina shuru mein jaane ke prey kaun hai.

Agar aisa ho, toh response shuru karne wala cue aur us response se faida paane wala animal alag ho sakte hain.

Calf defence se juda response kisi encounter mein seal ko bhi mauqa de sakta hai.

Lekin yeh proposed explanation hai—har encounter ka solved motive nahi. Researchers ne doosri species ko faida dene wali altruistic explanation ko bhi rule out nahi kiya.

Yahi farq important hai: kisi seal ko faida hua, yeh record karna ek baat hai. Whale ne jaan-boojh kar usi seal ko bachane ka faisla kiya, yeh establish karna doosri.

## 6 — Hunt se bada answer

Opening mein humne ek whale ko hunt ki taraf aate dekha tha.

Ab us action ke peeche ek possible chain nazar aati hai: approach ka cue, defensive behaviour, aur kabhi doosre animal ko milne wala escape ka mauqa.

Is chain ke saare links har encounter mein prove nahi hue. Review ki reports bhi duniya bhar se barabar nahi aayin; bohat se accounts Monterey Bay aur southeast Alaska ke the.

Lekin central observation clear hai: humpbacks kabhi orca hunts mein interfere karti hain. Isko samajhne ke liye sirf yeh poochhna kaafi nahi ke zyada powerful kaun hai.

Hunt ka outcome ek sawaal hai. Usmein aane ka motive doosra.

Humpback kabhi prey ko bachne ka mauqa de deti hai. Woh us hunt mein aayi kyun thi—isi ka poora jawab abhi baqi hai.

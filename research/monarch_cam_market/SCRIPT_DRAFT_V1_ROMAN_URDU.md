## 1 — Hunt ke beech mein

[Original illustrative reconstruction: orcas, potential prey aur approaching humpback ki spatial positions. No named date/location, no invented kill/escape. On-screen: “Illustrative reconstruction — not recorded footage”.]

Ek shikaar chal raha hai.

Orcas ke paas ek aur whale aa rahi hai. Lekin woh shikaar ka hissa banne nahi aa rahi. Woh humpback hai—krill aur chhoti machhliyan khane wali whale.

Humpbacks ko aise encounters mein orcas ki taraf aate, unka peecha karte, aur paani par apne flippers aur dum maarte record kiya gaya hai.

Kuch cases mein, orcas ka prey bach nikalta hai.

Isko dekh kar pehla lafz zehan mein aata hai: rescue.

Lekin kisi action se doosre jaanwar ko faida pahunchna, aur us action ka maqsad jaan-boojh kar usko bachana—yeh ek hi baat nahi.

Toh pehle maqsad ka faisla nahi karte. Pehle dekhte hain ke humpback ki entry hunt mein kya badal sakti hai.

[Evidence C01, C07, C08. “Kuch cases” escape statement S1 abstract; no claimed success rate. Opening payoff: observed interference vs inferred motive.]

## 2 — Disrupt karne ke liye maarna zaroori nahi

[Move into clearly schematic motion; no collision. Approaching animal aur hunters ke beech changing space show ho, scientifically reviewed action only.]

Yahan sawaal yeh nahi ke humpback ek orca ko hara sakti hai ya nahi.

Sawaal yeh hai: kya uski maujoodgi aur movement shikaar karne ka tareeqa badal deti hai?

Researchers ne aise encounters describe kiye jahan humpbacks approach karti hain, chase karti hain, zor se saans bahar nikalti hain, ya apne flippers aur dum se paani par display karti hain. Kuch encounters mein orcas unse bachti ya door hoti nazar aati hain.

Is behaviour ko mobbing kaha gaya.

Lekin flipper ka paani par lagna aur flipper ka kisi orca ko lagna alag observations hain. Hum pehli cheez dekh kar doosri assume nahi kar sakte.

Hunt disturb ho sakti hai bina iske ke koi predator mara jaye. Magar disturbance bhi har baar prey ko nahi bachati.

Ab ek ulta sawaal: agar adult humpback ke paas yeh defence hai, toh orcas humpbacks ko approach hi kyun karti hain?

[Evidence C01; disruption is qualified explanation, not measured energetic-cost result. No impact-force numbers or invincible-adult claim.]

## 3 — Adult ko dekh kar calf ka risk mat samjho

[Adult/calf spatial diagram. Orca diet distinction presented before hunting visuals; no graphic drowning reconstruction.]

Sab orca populations ek jaisi cheezen nahi khati. Kuch populations machhliyon par specialise karti hain; doosri marine mammals bhi hunt karti hain.

Humpbacks ke in encounters mein yeh distinction important hai.

Ek bade adult ke paas hona aur ek young whale ko target karna ek jaisa challenge nahi. Review ke attack accounts mein calves aur doosre young humpbacks important targets the. Kuch animals ki age clear nahi thi.

Isliye adult ki defensive movement aur young whale ki vulnerability ek saath sach ho sakti hain.

Yahan tak ek explanation samajh aati hai: humpbacks apni species ke young ko defend kar sakti hain.

Lekin records mein ek detail hai jo is answer ko adhura bana deti hai.

Har baar shikaar humpback nahi tha.

[Evidence C04, C09. Intent ko specific observed animal ki biography nahi banaya.]

## 4 — Jab shikaar doosri species ho

[Original evidence grouping, not fake incident footage. Labels: “Reported interactions”, “Prey identity known / unknown”.]

Pitman aur unke colleagues ne humpbacks aur killer whales ke 115 interactions ka review kiya.

Yeh 115 successful rescues ka collection nahi tha. Kuch encounters mein approach kisne ki, woh clear tha. Doosron mein observers pohanche toh interaction pehle se chal rahi thi.

Aur jahaan prey identify hua, usmein humpbacks ke saath seals, sea lions aur doosri species bhi thin.

Kuch accounts mein humpbacks ne hunt ke paas approach kiya. Lekin sirf paas hona aur actively interfere karna bhi ek hi cheez nahi. Researchers ne behaviours aur outcomes ko alag record kiya.

Table mein aise encounters bhi hain jahaan humpbacks maujood thin, displays bhi hue, aur phir bhi prey maara gaya.

Yeh detail kahani ko kam interesting nahi banati. Yeh sawaal ko zyada precise banati hai.

Agar har intervention successful nahi, aur har prey humpback nahi, toh humpback ko us jagah aane par trigger kya kar sakta hai?

## 5 — Awaaz sunna aur prey ko pehchanna

[Clearly labelled hypothesis diagram: hunting activity → possible acoustic cue → approach. Unknown prey identity marked unknown. No measured sound radius.]

Researchers ne ek possibility propose ki: humpbacks attacking mammal-eating orcas ki awaazon par respond kar sakti hain, bina shuru mein yeh jaane ke shikaar kaun hai.

Yahan do sawaal alag ho jaate hain.

Pehla: whale approach karna shuru kyun karti hai?

Doosra: us approach se faida kisko hota hai?

Ho sakta hai ek response jo calf defence ke context mein useful ho, kisi encounter mein doosri species ko bhi faida de.

Lekin yeh hypothesis hai. Har humpback ki har approach ka proved explanation nahi.

Aur is possibility ka matlab yeh bhi nahi ke deliberate help impossible hai. Paper ne doosri species ko faida dene wali altruistic explanation ko rule out nahi kiya.

Hum behaviour dekh sakte hain. Hum outcome record kar sakte hain. Lekin whale ke zehan mein wahi intention tha jo hamein scene dekh kar mehsoos hua—uske liye alag evidence chahiye.

[Evidence C07. No mind-reading, revenge, moral verdict or established kin relationship.]

## 6 — Record hamein kya batata hai, aur kya nahi

[Return to opening's spatial model in a new moving explanatory state. Three labels introduced separately: action, outcome, motive. Not a repeated still.]

115 interactions ka matlab poore samundar ka census bhi nahi.

Is review ke bohat se accounts Monterey Bay aur southeast Alaska se aaye. Jo encounters dekhe aur report kiye gaye, unse hum patterns samajh sakte hain. Lekin unko har jagah, har population, har hunt ka universal rule nahi bana sakte.

Toh opening ke scene mein ab teen layers alag nazar aani chahiye.

Humpback ka approach aur interference: observed behaviour.

Prey ka bachna ya maara jaana: encounter ka outcome.

Humpback ne aisa kyun kiya: explanation jiske kuch hisse supported hain, aur kuch abhi uncertain.

Humpbacks kabhi orca hunts mein dakhal deti hain. Kuch cases mein isse prey ko bachne ka mauqa milta hai. Calf defence aur possible sound-triggered response is behaviour ko samajhne ke raaste dete hain—magar doosri species wale cases poori kahani ko simple nahi rehne dete.

Isliye sahi sawaal sirf yeh nahi ke kis whale ne kis whale ko hara diya.

Sahi sawaal yeh hai ke humne kya hote dekha—aur us observation se hum kitni door tak conclusion nikaal sakte hain.

[Evidence C02, C03, C07. Main promise closed; no sequel-gated answer. Optional CTA not written; ending review pending.]

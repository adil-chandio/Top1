## Source-text constraints carried forward

Specimens were fixed splayed/prone and preserved; detailed geometry derives from six adults, not a live kinematic capture. Digit I is clawless and differs in distal skeletal arrangement from II–V. Width/count varies by digit and individual; alternative OB/TB/LDT definitions cannot be summed into one mandated ridge count or adhesive boundary. Left/right can differ. The article's length/width criterion wording differs between methods and results; no numeric asset threshold is inferred from that phrase.

Article-level CC BY 4.0 is recorded, but figure-specific credit/reuse clearance remains unverified. Images remain research references, not approved production plates/textures. Anatomy evidence is not motion evidence or rights approval.

## Acceptance checklist before the bounded integrated proof

1. Identify species, left hindfoot, digit labels and viewing side for each reference; keep manuscripts/forefeet, right feet and dissections explicitly separate.
2. Obtain and actually inspect external oblique/side context that includes ankle/limb, digit terminal/claw and substrate. Record source/provenance and reuse restrictions. Fixed sections cannot fill this gap by invented dimensional conversion.
3. Author exterior with source-supported width variation, distinct distal zones, plausible claw integration and connected limb context; compare silhouette in ventral AND intended camera view. No claim of measured geometry without measurements.
4. Specify qualitative motion controls separately from bones/tendons; no equal-link biological inference, universal release angle/time or full gait claim.
5. Main release must read through opaque silhouette and growing physical gap with labels/tint off, continuously across S08/S09. This remains an untested design requirement.

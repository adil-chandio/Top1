## Actually opened, not caption-only

Three search-delivered photographs were opened with the image viewer; local hashes and retained ignored runtime paths are in `CONTEXT_REFERENCE_INSPECTION.json`. The two EarthDate copies are 480×319, limiting fine anatomy and contact judgement. Copies are not authenticated original bytes.

1. **EarthDate flat state:** https://www.earthdate.org/episodes/hanging-with-geckos (both chunks reviewed). The picture shows an oblique limb terminating at glass, with dorsal scales/orange markings, multiple digits directed along the plane and visible reflected foot. Broad external pads are not visible in the same way as the lifted image. This is not proof of contact area/force or that every pad is attached.
2. **EarthDate hyperextended state:** same page, image path `files/inline-images/gecko%20toe%20hyperextended%20flat%20on%20a%20glass%20surface.jpg`. Visible ventral transverse ridges wrap around curled tips, with the limb and glass/reflection still providing spatial context. Several visible digits curl differently; do not impose identical synchronized rigid-link rotations on every toe. The actual glass-gap boundary is not sufficiently resolved to measure it. The page captions the state as hyperextended to release, but this is a still, not an observed release sequence.
3. **Commons ventral photo:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gecko_foot_on_glass.JPG . The opened copy shows paired/divided-looking transverse pad structures, surface scales and an ankle/limb on glass. Do NOT transfer its divided pad appearance into Tokay geometry: the file description does not establish species, side or manus/pes identity. This is a contextual comparator, not the exterior anatomy master.

## Concrete treatment consequence

Use a located oblique exterior establishing view where limb, foot and substrate plane coexist; only then move to the digit-local insert. Flat-versus-curled appearance must be legible from opaque physical surfaces, not color alone. Keep clear visual distinction between real pad underside and its reflection; do not use a mirror image as evidence of a second contacting foot or a measured gap. These are staging decisions, NOT permission to manufacture a biologically validated asset.

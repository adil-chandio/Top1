## Evidence aur animation constraints

1. **Directional frictional adhesion**: Autumn et al. 2006, JEB209:3569–3579, DOI10.1242/jeb.02486. Stanford author PDF full text chunks0–8 read. Proximal shear supports attachment. Ordinary sticky-tape model alone insufficient. Isolated seta critical angle around30°, array around25°: these are NOT toe joint angles. Whole-toe slow tilt is not running gait. Model prediction is not a filmed universal gait.
Source: http://bdml.stanford.edu/twiki/pub/Main/ClimbingAdhesionPublications/Autumn_et_al._-_2006_-_Frictional_adhesion_A_new_angle_on_gecko_attachment..pdf

2. **Isolated arrays, not commanded whole-foot path**: Gravish et al. DOI10.1098/rsif.2007.1077, article chunks0–8 read (last bibliography chunk not read). Detachment-path theta, setal shaft alpha, and skeletal joint angle are distinct. 130° low-work array trajectory must NOT become a gecko joint animation command. Negative isolated-array work does not mean zero animal metabolic cost. Figure retrieval still pending; captions are not pixels.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2607396/

3. **Active tip-first release corroborated**: Higham & Russell2025 JEB228:jeb247980, author PDF chunks0–2 of10 read. Distal digits actively hyperextend before foot lift on inclines. Figure2 caption describes extensor digitorum brevis/dorsal interossei pulling the penultimate phalanx; lateral digital tendon branches transmit flexor tension to scansors; long flexor drives claw toward substrate. Caption read only, figure pixels NOT inspected. Complex musculotendon/vascular integration must not be reduced to a literal on/off button. Species diversity and dynamic control remain research gaps. Do not use historical H.garnotii20ms as universal tokay timing.
Source: https://biomechanics.ucr.edu/Higham-Russell%202025.pdf

4. **Tokay-specific foot/ankle guardrail**: Higham, Zhuang & Russell2021, Journal of Anatomy239:1503–1515, author PDF chunks0–1 of8 read. Tokay mesotarsal morphology supports pedal reorientation partly decoupled from flexion/extension. Figure1 caption identifies actual tokay dorsal/ventral reference views, but pixels NOT inspected. Digits fan over broad arc and have subequal lengths; do not make a human hand with a gecko texture. Text describes pads hyperextending before ankle plantarflexion on climbing surfaces. Level glass example keeps distal digits hyperextended throughout cycle: context-specific observation, not all surfaces/species. Static figure angles148°/29° describe foot orientation, not time-series toe bends. Exact anatomy/rig still unapproved.
Source: https://biomechanics.ucr.edu/Higham%20et%20al%202021%20JAnat.pdf

## Hardest-shot technical acceptance criteria

Create original, explicitly schematic articulated single digit before building whole animal. Contact phase → distal-first hyperextension → proximal progression → clear detached state. Show substrate, toe pad and contact region separately. Initial joint timings/angles are illustrative, NOT measured tokay gait. Preserve segment lengths; no substrate penetration, no instantaneous whole-pad teleport, no claw as suction cup. Inspect consecutive frames at release front, not a single attractive pose. A schematic technical test cannot pass final species anatomy or natural gait QC.

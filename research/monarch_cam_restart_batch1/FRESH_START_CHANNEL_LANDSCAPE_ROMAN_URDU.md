## Sequence aur exit checks
1. Broad landscape → primary channel identity + promise/catalogue screen.
2. Useful diverse channels ki reasoned selection; availability ko quality samajh kar choose nahin.
3. Per-channel separate format/subtype/date cohorts; best/middle/low examples, reliable baseline or N/A.
4. Original titles + actual thumbnail images inspect; packaging observations, causal claims nahin.
5. Topic clusters/repeated signals/confounders → opportunity map.
6. Is evidence ke baad hi top3 original ideas; reasons/risks/title-thumbnail direction and recommendation.

Deep cuts/camera/animation analysis Batch4 ka hai. Yeh document **first discovery checkpoint** hai, complete Batch1 ya substantial final research delivery nahin. Pending primary screening ko researched channel finding nahin ginna.

## Jo seekhna hai, jo copy nahin karna
1. **Semantic editing:** naya shot/tabdeeli tab lao jab scale, pathway ya question badle. Har chand seconds arbitrary cut lagana reference ka asal lesson nahin.
2. **Context ko ghost karo, gayab nahin:** focus part chamakta hai, baqi machine faint rehti hai; viewer ko dobara location nahin dhoondhni parti.
3. **Ek unit → connected system → whole payoff:** topic engine lock nahin; yeh visual explanation ka transferable structure hai.
4. **Graphic labels narration ke badal nahin:** sampled frames mein labels component names/steps hain. Inhen har lafz ke captions ke saath confuse nahin karna.
5. **Monarch Cam ke liye adaptation:** ek short mein poora 8-minute subsystem syllabus nahin bharna. Ek causal mystery, limited visual vocabulary aur end par resolved whole choose karna—final topic baqi references ke baad.
6. **Avoid:** mobile par chhote labels ka exact legibility verdict contact-sheet thumbnails se nahin. Original scale crop/dense review ke baghair typography ko perfect kehna ghalat hoga.

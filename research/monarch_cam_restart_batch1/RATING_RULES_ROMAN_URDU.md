## Har final shortlisted idea ka scorecard

| Dimension | Weight | Kis sawal ka jawab |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 15% | Pehle chand seconds mein clear, surprising sawal banta hai? |
| Story/payoff | 15% | Sirf fact nahi; causal progression aur satisfying answer hai? |
| Relative performance | 20% | Same-channel, same-format comparable cohort ke against evidence kya hai? |
| Visual clarity | 10% | Behaviour screen par pehchana aur samjhaya ja sakta hai? |
| Fresh angle | 10% | Search sample mein angle kitna repeated hai; hamara distinction kya hai? |
| Actual AV execution reference | 15% | Viewed/heard reference se editing, pacing, sound aur packaging ka kya saboot hai? |
| Preliminary feasibility | 10% | Sahi species aur plausibly licensable visual route hai? Detailed production proof Batch2 mein. |
| Monarch Cam fit | 5% | Roman Urdu cinematic animal-behaviour story ke liye relevant hai? |

Final score = sum(score_out_of_10 × weight)/100. Yeh editorial decision hai, virality ki probability nahi. 8/10 ka matlab80% viral chance nahi.

## Score anchors

- **0–2:** evidence criterion ke khilaf strong objection dikhata hai.
- **3–4:** weak case; important drawbacks.
- **5–6:** workable, mixed evidence.
- **7–8:** strong, specific supporting evidence aur manageable drawbacks.
- **9:** exceptionally strong demonstrated case; rare.
- **10:** criterion ke defined sample mein outstanding case; perfection ka universal claim nahi.
- **N/A:** evidence missing. Zero nahi; guessed5 bhi nahi.

Har numeric rating ke saath **reason, source/observation aur low/medium/high confidence** zaroori. Unknown dimensions ke weights redistribute nahi honge. Missing AV ya cohort ke saath total/rank publish nahi hoga. `scorecard.py` yahi enforce karta hai.

## Performance comparison ke rules

- Shorts aur long videos separate. Compilations, livestreams, reactions aur originals separate cohorts.
- Target ke around same channel/format ke10 comparables ka aim; minimum5 usable comparables. Exclusions aur sample size disclose honge. Is se kam par rating hold.
- Median comparison = target views / comparable median. Yeh retention, causal success ya algorithm endorsement prove nahi karta.
- Upload age prominently report; lifetime views/day ko current velocity nahi kahenge. Different-age videos ka ratio raw descriptive ratio hoga, age-adjusted uplift nahi.
- Rounded13K ko exact13000 bana kar precision imply nahi karenge.
- Search sample mein kam hits = entire platform low saturation ka proof nahi.

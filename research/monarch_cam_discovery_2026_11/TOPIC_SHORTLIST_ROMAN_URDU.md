## Shortlist 1 — weapon ke saath natural protection

**Working title:** 'Is Shrimp Ko Apne Hi Blast Se Kaun Bachata Hai?'

**Original hook candidate:** 'Apne panje se yeh shrimp paani mein shock wave banata hai. Phir apne hi vaar se iska dimaagh kaise bachta hai?'

**Scientific base:**
- 2022 Current Biology study: Alpheus heterochaelis ka helmet-like orbital hood shock waves damp karta hai. Publisher fulltext extraction ad/navigation shell bani; substantive 2022 findings search excerpt aur university report se mile. Full paper read ka claim nahi. https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(22)01004-1
- 2025 Journal of Experimental Biology: abstract actually padha. Intact aur removed hood animals ki tested spatial visual acuity mein difference nahi mila. Isko 'perfect vision', 'har shrimp same' ya 'kabhi visually impaired nahi' nahi bana sakte. Publisher version date 26 June 2025 hai; issue/citation date 15 June bhi dikhati hai. https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/228/12/jeb250592/368407/Shock-wave-damping-orbital-hoods-cover-the-eyes-of
- 2026 Royal Society Interface: publisher metadata, abstract aur discussion subsection actually padha. Online publication 11 February 2026; issue date 1 February alag hai. Mechanical tests aur finite-element model. Neural tissue strain/stress reduction 28%/22% **simulation predictions** hain, measured human helmet benefits nahi. Multiple protective mechanisms discuss hue; water expulsion ko sole proven mechanism nahi banayenge. https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsif/article/23/235/20250769/480253/Structural-and-mechanical-properties-facilitate
- **Fresh peg: 29 October 2026 Scientific Reports.** Abstract, rights aur publication metadata actually padha. Shock-wave-exposed shrimp without hoods ne intact-hood shrimp ke muqable slower, longer, more tortuous shelter-seeking paths liye. Short-term behavioral evidence hai, permanent brain injury ya total immunity ka direct proof nahi. Main methods/sample tables returned page mein nahi mile; sample size/effect-size claims abhi withheld. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-77517-2

**Audience signal:**

## Shortlist 2 — paani ka jet jo target ke paas focus hota hai

**Working title:** 'Yeh Machhli Paani Ke Jet Ko Target Ke Paas Kaise Jama Karti Hai?'

Direct Cell fetch ad shell; ScienceDaily/Cell Press aur Science News corroboration read via search result. Full primary methods read nahi. https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(14)00922-1

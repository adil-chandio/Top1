## Original narration

Yeh shrimp paani mein shock wave banata hai. Phir apne hi vaar se iska dimaagh kaise bachta hai?

Panja band hote hi paani ka tez jet nikalta hai. Ek cavitation bubble banta hai. Bubble collapse hota hai, to pressure wave nikalti hai.

Lekin is shrimp ki aankhon ke upar ek transparent cover hai: orbital hood. Yeh iske shell ka hissa hai, jo shock wave ka asar kam karta hai.

Researchers ne hood wale aur hood ke baghair shrimp ko shock waves ke baad compare kiya. Hood ke baghair shrimp panah tak pohanchne mein zyada ghoome, zyada door chale aur dheere chale.

Yeh poori immunity nahi. Hood wave ko khatam nahi karta—uska asar kam karta hai.

## Camera/art direction

- Correct species silhouette and claw mechanics before mood. No generic lobster claw, mantis-shrimp club or inaccurate eye placement.
- Motivated macro perspective with a scale relationship, not endless floating collage layers.
- Subject readable against habitat; no extreme darkness or empty frame repeating old orca blocking failure.
- Explanatory shell/hood transparency can be selective and clearly conceptual. No false claim that this animal's normally visible brain has been filmed.
- Avoid manufactured large smoke, glowing fireball, energy shield or sci-fi helmet. 'Helmet-like' is a biological analogy, not worn equipment.
- Typography optional: one short identification at a time. No wall of English headings; viewer-facing text Roman Urdu except scientific name/term as needed.
- A still photograph is a legitimate anatomy/reference asset, **not final articulated motion**. No pan/zoom-still substitute for required animation.

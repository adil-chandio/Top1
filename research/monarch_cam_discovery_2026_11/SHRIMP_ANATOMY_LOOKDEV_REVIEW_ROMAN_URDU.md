# Shrimp — anatomy aur macro visual test ka actual review

## Is batch mein kya actually hua

1. JEB2019 ka species-specific Fig1 thumbnail acquire karke khola. Uska publisher caption bhi padha. PanelB mein compound eyes ke upar transparent hood aur anterior margin ke arrowheads dikhte hain. Yeh pehle wali dark lateral specimen photo se zyada informative hai.
2. 2022 research ki Phys.org coverage ka three-panel photograph khola. Head close-up mein eye ke aage/uper shell boundary ka relation nazar aata hai. Yeh scientific reference hai, cleared production plate nahi.
3. Licensed Commons original ko reference dekar ek naya AI macro look-development frame banaya. Frame khol kar dekha, phir anatomy-focused corrective edit banaya aur usay bhi khola.
4. **Dono generated frames final/hero use ke liye reject kiye.** Photographic texture aur lighting ko anatomical correctness ka substitute nahi maana.

## Source-specific inspection

### R1 — JEB2019, Vision in the snapping shrimp Alpheus heterochaelis

Publisher: https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/222/21/jeb209015/224469/Vision-in-the-snapping-shrimp-Alpheus

DOI: 10.1242/jeb.209015.

Actually accessed: publisher chunk2, Fig1 caption + adjacent introduction + start of animal collection methods. Thumbnail actual opened: `image-search/alpheus-heterochaelis-orbital-hoods-eyes-3.png`.

- PanelA dorsal whole-animal view; claw asymmetry readable.
- PanelB shows paired dark compound-eye areas through the surrounding translucent head covering; caption explicitly identifies hood anterior margin.
- PanelC enlarged compound facets; not an exposed-brain image.
- Caption scale bars: A1cm, B500micrometres, C50micrometres. **In alag panels ka scale ek dusre par ya Commons photo par apply nahi kiya.**
- Is thumbnail ko scientific spatial relation samajhne ke liye use kiya, na trace kiya, na production assets mein copy kiya. Commercial/adapted figure reuse clearance establish nahi hui.

## Generated test v1 — reject

File: `assets/shrimp-macro-lookdev-v1.png`.

- Underwater macro lighting, sediment separation aur material detail ka original test bana; image AI-generated hai, filmed animal nahi.
- Visible eye spherical/protruding read hoti hai; protective covering ka required relation convincingly establish nahi hota.
- Claw arrangement aur relative mass species-specific snapping mechanism ko confidently communicate nahi karte.
- **Decision:** na hero plate, na hood reveal, na anatomical evidence. Is still se pan/zoom video nahi banani.

## Corrective test v2 — reject

File: `assets/shrimp-macro-lookdev-v2.png`.

- Correction ne shell ko literal clear visor jaisa bana diya: overly distinct panel-like edges aur broad transparent forehead cover.
- Claw asymmetry ka correction credible snapped-claw structure resolve nahi karta. Head design species ke confidently verified close-up jaisa nahi.
- **Decision:** v1 ka replacement approve nahi. Iterations yahin bounded roki; incorrect image par motion add karke defect chhupana nahi.

## Production mein ab kya badla

- Hero macro ke liye generic lobster eye stalks aur separate glass-helmet silhouette explicit rejection criteria hain.
- Hood reveal mein shell continuity aur eye-under-cover relation pehle prove hona chahiye; decorative outline kaafi nahi.
- Species identity/reference route ab whole-animal Commons photo + independent scientific head image tak pohanch chuki. Exact 3D geometry, clearable hero model, articulated animation aur final soundtrack abhi approved nahi.
- Original narration abhi wahi evidence-bounded115-word draft hai. Is test ki wajah se koi naya biological claim add nahi hua.
- Koi video export, Drive video upload, actual hearing ya viral-YouTube AV review is batch mein claim nahi hua.

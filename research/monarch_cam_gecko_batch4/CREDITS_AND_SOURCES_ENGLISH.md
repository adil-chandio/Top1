## Visual interpretation notice

All 3D models and animation are original **qualitative schematics**. Toe proportions, external skin warp, claw placeholders, setal counts, branching layout, spacing and animation timing are illustrative, not measured reconstructions. Microscale structures are enlarged/simplified for explanation. A loading arrow shows direction, not force magnitude. Contact indicators are not empirical setal contact-area or adhesive-stress data. No vacuum chamber, liquid glue or microscopic spring bonds are asserted.

## Comparative judgment — what Gecko needs beyond cosmetic V7
This is a reasoned treatment direction, NOT production approval or a claim that current assets can meet the reference quality.

1. **One causal question per view.** Pencil's component motion, Microchips' fixed substrate and Storm's fixed junction all let changed geometry carry meaning. Gecko release needs a readable contact boundary shrinking during tip-first hyperextension, not camera movement standing in for detachment.
2. **A continuous scale ladder.** Whole climbing animal→identified foot→identified toe/pad→lamellae/setal schematic→same toe returning to gait. Keep side, surface normal and travel direction consistent; locator/inset or a recognizable landmark must survive scale changes.
3. **Anatomy and schematic visibly separate.** No unsupported realistic claw/joint mechanics; no array angle promoted to a whole-toe command, suction cup, zero-energy claim or universal20ms timing. Science ledger still binds the rig.
4. **Release shot beats before decoration.** Establish loaded contact, cue active tip lift, show progressive reduction in contact, then clearance/replant. Hold oblique view long enough to read cause/effect; camera translation only after the mechanism is clear. Exact timing remains to be grounded, not copied from engineering references.
5. **Color is a contract.** Neutral anatomy; one consistent local contact highlight; distinct schematic force cue only if justified. Do not paint biological structures as literal manufactured layers or copy Branch's signature environment/model layout.
6. **Real state changes, not slideshow cards.** Macro toe motion, local contact-state change and return to whole-foot context must be actual authored connected action. More labels, glossy floor, particles, fast cuts or sponsor-style montage will not remedy the rejected baseline.
7. **Editing and sound still need separate evidence.** These sheets support visual construction only. No sound design/music/voice sync conclusions. English voice-09 remains selected; actual listening/AV review is still required.

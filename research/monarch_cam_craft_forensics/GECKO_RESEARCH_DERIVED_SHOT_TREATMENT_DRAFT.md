## Creative proposition
Make the viewer follow one toe from visible attachment to visible clearance. The reveal is a change in contact geometry, not a replacement title or an orbit. Repeatedly return to the same toe and surface so the microscopic explanation answers the opening movement rather than becoming an unrelated particle sequence.

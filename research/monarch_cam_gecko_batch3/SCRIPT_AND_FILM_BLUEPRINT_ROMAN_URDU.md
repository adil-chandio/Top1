## Title / thumbnail promise

- Working title: **Gecko chipakta hai… phir chhootta kaise hai?**
- Thumbnail concept: one foot closeup, distal tip visibly raised while proximal pad remains in contact; short text **“PHIR CHHOOTTA KAISE?”**
- Image must not depict glue strings, suction cups, magical glowing bonds or a different species' foot. Thumbnail is a future original production asset, not yet generated or selected. No click-through performance claim.

## Voiceover — continuous reading draft

Gecko deewar par chipka rehta hai. Lekin phir har qadam par us ka paon chhootta kaise hai?

Raaz sirf mazboot pakar nahin. Pakarne aur chhorne ka tareeqa bhi hai.

Tokay gecko ki ungliyon ke neeche bareek baalon jaisi structures hoti hain: setae. In ke sire mazeed chhoti shaakhon mein bantte hain, jo satah ke bohat qareeb contact banati hain.

Yeh suction cups nahin. Is qareebi contact mein van der Waals forces hissa leti hain.

Aur kheenchne ki direction aham hai. Ungli ke base ki taraf shear, yani satah ke saath lagne wala kheenchao, grip ko support karta hai.

Chhorne ke waqt ungli ka agla hissa pehle peeche ki taraf murta hai. Contact tip se base ki taraf dheere-dheere chhootta hai; poora pad ek saath nahin uthta.

Is movement ko digital hyperextension kehte hain. Yeh ungli ki harkat hai—ise microscopic baalon ke angle se confuse na karein.

Toh gecko har qadam par bas zor laga kar paon nahin ukhaarta. Us ki pakar mein contact, kheenchao ki direction, aur ungli ki harkat—teenon ka kirdar hai.

## Art / camera / typography

- Macro environment: neutral dark blue-grey substrate; warm natural pad colour. No suction disks or luminescent adhesive coating. Green, if used at all, is clearly labelled contact overlay, not skin colour/force heatmap.

- Provisional clean sans-serif; choose production font only after checking bundled font licence. Roman Urdu captions limited to key phrases; mobile-safe placement and legibility QC required.

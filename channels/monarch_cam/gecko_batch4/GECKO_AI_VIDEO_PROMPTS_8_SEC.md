# Gecko film — eight 8-second AI video prompts

**Format:** 8 sequential clips × 8 seconds = 64 seconds; vertical 9:16. Visuals only: no voiceover, dialogue, music, sound effects, captions, labels, logos, or text. Generate Clip 1 first, then use the final frame of each finished clip as the reference/start frame for the next clip whenever the AI tool supports it. Keep the same seed and character reference when supported. If continuity or an anatomy detail changes, regenerate that clip before continuing. For a scale change, the reference frame is an art-direction/colour reference; match on the toe or surface and make a deliberate cut in editing if the tool cannot make that transition convincingly. This approach encourages but cannot guarantee consistent identity or anatomically accurate motion.

**Character and style for every clip:** a believable adult Tokay gecko, blue-grey pebbled skin with muted orange spots, broad head, golden eyes with vertical pupils, four natural articulated limbs, five splayed toes per foot and wide adhesive toe pads. The same animal and markings throughout. Dark, damp vertical stone wall in a night forest; deep teal shadows and soft warm rim lighting; cinematic, realistic high-end wildlife CGI with realistic physical depth and shallow depth of field. Educational animal film, not cartoon. The toe and contact area must be clearly visible when shown. No extra limbs or digits, distorted anatomy, flickering textures, floating feet, sudden morphing, exaggerated movement, suction cups, visible glue or magnets.

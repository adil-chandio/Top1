## Department-level verdict, bounded to inspected evidence
- **Staging/art:** coherent room→isolatedcutaway→room; warm neutral wall vs saturated moving parts. Stronger world/context than our isolated brown bar, though more labels are not automatically more premium.
- **Materials/light:** desk scene has distinguishable wall/metal/plastic and shadow depth; cutaway uses selective highlights and transparency to separate concentric parts. Fine shader/texture quality cannot be judged at200pxsheetcells; no renderer/shaderreverseengineering claim.

## Concrete gecko revision rules learned
1. Start with an animal/environment interaction, not a title sitting over a detachedfoot silhouette.
2. Keep surface,foot orientation and attachedcontact context readable through the scale change.
3. Use sparse,local annotations to explain contact and loading; do not fill every frame with labels.
4. Spend motion on the actual mechanism/statechange. Do not disguise a rigidblock as articulated animalmotion.
5. Return to wholeanimal action for payoff; no generic “three principles” slide as substitute.
6. Maintain an actual observedflaw/limit column even for benchmarkfilms. Good references are not flawless and their science is not automatically transferred.

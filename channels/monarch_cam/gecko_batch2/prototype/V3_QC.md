# Gecko concept V3 — QC and limitations

## What was built

`gecko-concept-v3.mp4`: 12 seconds, 640×360, 8 fps, 96 H.264 frames; mono AAC 22.05 kHz. Four conceptual shots: generic stylized gecko, foot close-up, enlarged single toe underside with lamella-like rows, and branching seta-like strands contacting a surface. An original synthesized chord bed is included; no voiceover or natural SFX. Original geometry and audio are generated by project scripts, no external model/video/audio asset is included. Rough review preview only.

## Checks actually performed

- FFmpeg decoded all 96 frames and the audio stream without errors. The 96 source PNGs passed Pillow verification. The four caption text widths are 451–482 px within the 596 px available. FFmpeg `volumedetect` reported mean -28.4 dB and peak -20.6 dB; not a loudness standard or perceptual quality assessment.
- Inspected stills from each new shot, including micro frames 0 and 18 of 24. Source has slight sideways movement of the upper lamella relative to fixed tips. It is a deliberately designed conceptual deformation, **not** a measured gecko experiment or simulated adhesion. I did not directly listen to the soundtrack or watch continuous playback; synchronisation, audible sound quality and the perception of smoothness remain unverified.
- Relevant claims are supported at a broad level by [Autumn et al. 2006](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/209/18/3569/16118/Frictional-adhesion-a-new-angle-on-gecko), [Tian et al. 2006](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1748224/), and [Russell et al. 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13617260/). The 30-degree threshold is not a universal joint angle. Not every gecko has adhesive pads.

## Self-review — rejected as final / Batch 2 still open

The new toe image communicates rows but remains a pill with raised stripes, not a convincing gecko digit; the macro shot resembles a mechanical brush or scaffold, with unrealistically sparse oversized fibers and a rectangular pad. The caption bar obscures the lowest parts of some tips. Body is cartoonish, toe close-up is not validated and does not show natural contact or climbing; there is no natural gait or detachment. Eight frames per second is not production-level motion. V3's extra shot and lateral movement do not validate anatomy or constitute a working physics model. Audio, high-performing actual 3D benchmark, and a production-grade model remain open. Do not describe this as final quality, continuous listening, or a Batch 2 pass.

## Reproduction

Run `build_gecko_shots.py`, `build_toe_study.py` and `build_seta_motion.py` with Blender-compatible Python/bpy and appropriate platform libraries; then run `assemble_v3.py` with Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg installed. The intermediate frames and Blender executables are not committed. FFmpeg is provided through imageio-ffmpeg. The scripts use local DejaVu Sans paths. No paid assets were purchased.

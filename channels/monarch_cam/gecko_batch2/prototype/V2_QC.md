# Gecko prototype v2 QC — 18 November 2026

9-second rough concept, 640×360, 8fps, 72 frames, H.264 with mono AAC; full stream decoded successfully through ffmpeg. Original simple synthesised chord bed, no narration, natural sounds or SFX. No listening tool was available, so audio is technically present but its audible quality and balance were not independently judged.

Changes from v1: toe pad bottoms moved to the surface plane, rigid-body sideways sliding removed, camera performs a mild move instead, sampling doubled from 4fps to 8fps, and the earlier rigid-translation macro animation was replaced by a stationary diagram with a digital push-in. Three resulting stills were visually inspected. The foot reads closer to the surface, but exact contact and intersection were not numerically validated; its shape is still cartoonish and the caption bar partly obscures the lower foot. The model is a generic stylization, not a validated tokay. The macro diagram remains a brush-like approximation and cannot show the actual density, scale, directional loading or release of gecko setae. No actual climbing, gait or scientifically reliable micro-motion is depicted. Eight fps remains low and motion may look choppy; this is not final quality or a Batch 2 pass.

Sources: `build_gecko_shots.py` makes current v2 frames using bpy; `assemble_v2.py` builds the 72-frame preview and original synthesized bed with Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg, and reads `../micro_contact_proof/frame-10.png`. No third-party models or sound assets used. Video is a rough private review prototype, not published as finished science.

## 1. LOCKED SCRIPT (`ep01_pehra.txt` — 240 words, 16 beats)

1. **Pehra kis cheez par tha?**
2. **Is aadmi ne apni hi fasal chori karwane ke liye khet ke bahar fauji khade kar diye.**
3. Satrah sau chhiyasi me France bhookh se mar raha tha.
4. Ek scientist Parmentier ke paas us bhookh ka seedha ilaj tha, aaloo.
5. Magar logon ne kaha ke aaloo janwaron ka kachra hai, aur isse bimaari phailti hai.
6. Usne sadak par aaloo ki tokriyan bina daam rakhin, magar bhookhe logon ne haath tak nahi lagaya.
7. Tab Parmentier ne samjhana band kiya.
8. Usne badshah se Paris ke bahar chaalis acre ka khet maanga, aur wahan aaloo uga diye.
9. Phir poore khet ke gird bandook wale shahi fauji khade kar diye.
10. Din bhar log deewar ke bahar ruk kar poochte: andar aisa kaunsa khazana hai jis par badshah ka pehra hai?
11. Shaam hui to Parmentier ne faujiyon ke kaan me aakhri hukm diya.
12. Sooraj doobte hi chupchaap ghar chale jaana, aur raat ko koi deewar phande to rokna mat.
13. Usi raat log andhere me khet me ghuse, aur wohi aaloo chura kar bhaage jise kal thook rahe the.
14. Chand hafton me poora khet khaali ho gaya, har ghar ke choolhe par aaloo chadha, aur laakhon jaanein bach gayin.
15. Aaj do sau saal baad bhi Paris me uski qabar ke gird log aaloo ke paude ugate hain.
16. **Jis cheez par tum pehra nahi lagate, duniya use sadak ka kachra samajhti hai, chahe woh tumhara hunar ho, tumhara waqt ho, ya tum khud.**

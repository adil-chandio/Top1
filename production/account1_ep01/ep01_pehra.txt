Is aadmi ne khet me aaloo ugaye, aur bahar bandook wale fauji khade kar diye.

Chori rokne ke liye nahi — taake log khud aakar chura lein.

Ajeeb laga na? Magar is ek chaal ne poore mulk ki jaan bacha li.

Do sau saal pehle France me log sadkon par bhookh se mar rahe the.

Ek scientist ke paas iska sabse aasaan hal tha — aaloo.

Masla bas yeh tha ke log aaloo ko janwaron ka khana samajhte the, aur darte the ke isse bimaari lag jayegi.

Usne aaloo ki bhari tokriyan bazaar me free rakh din — ek bande ne haath tak nahi lagaya.

Tab usne samjhana chhoda, aur logon ke dimagh ke saath ek chhota sa game khela.

Usne badshah se Paris ke bahar ek bada khet liya, wahan aaloo lagaye, aur chaaron taraf shahi fauji khade kar diye.

Ab jo wahan se guzarta, ruk kar jhaankta: yaar andar aisa kya ug raha hai jis par badshah ki fauj pehra de rahi hai?

Do din me poore shehar me bas usi khet ke charche hone lage.

Aur phir aaya is game ka sabse mazedar twist.

Usne faujiyon ke kaan me kaha: din bhar poori sakhti dikhana, magar sooraj doobte hi chupchaap ghar chale jaana — aur raat ko koi chori kare, to aankhein band kar lena.

Usi raat log deewar kood kar andar ghuse, aur boriyan bhar kar wohi aaloo chura le gaye jinhe subah free me koi pooch bhi nahi raha tha.

Kuch hafton me poora khet chori ho gaya, har ghar me aaloo pakne laga, aur laakhon logon ki jaan bach gayi.

Aur sabse mazedar baat? Aaj do sau saal baad bhi Paris me log us scientist Parmentier ki qabar par phool nahi — aaloo rakh kar aate hain.

Insaan ka dimagh aaj bhi waisa hi hai: jo cheez aasaani se mil jaye woh kachra lagti hai, aur jis par pehra laga ho, woh khazana.

Ab sach batao — tumne khud ko logon ke liye kitna aasaan bana rakha hai?

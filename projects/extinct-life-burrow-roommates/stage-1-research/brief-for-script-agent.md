## Winner concept

**Hook:** “Two animals shared one burrow for 250 million years.”  
**Paradox / “Wait.” beat:** the amphibian was not invited in — it was injured, and the burrow owner was asleep.

Proof beats (all original 3D, no reused footage):
1. Locked burrow cast, 9:16 vertical, one bright X-ray sweep.
2. Sagittal cutaway reveals two skeletons lying side by side.
3. Macro on partially healed ribs; no tooth marks.
4. Sleeping *Thrinaxodon* inferred from curled-posture fossils; mark as inference.
5. Flood infill seals the pair; return to the intact cast for loop.

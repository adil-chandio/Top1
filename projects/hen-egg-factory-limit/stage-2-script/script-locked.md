## Stage B — Claws autopsy

Benchmark: `cDXrpGbp34c` (SP CLASSES, 6,694,593 views, 149 s, 2,138x). Full transcript read. Hindi narration, so no line is reused.

**What it does right**
- Five-station conveyor tour: ovary → infundibulum → magnum → isthmus → uterus. Each station gets a name, a timer and a function, so the viewer always knows where the egg is.
- One hard number per station: 15–20 min, 3 h, 1–2 h, 18–20 h. Numbers carry retention.
- Closes with the myth-buster: eggs are laid without a rooster.

**Where it leaks**
- The real limit is buried. It spends 130 s touring and one sentence on the fact that 18–20 hours of shell calcification is why the hen cannot produce more than roughly one egg a day.
- No stakes. Calcium cost is never framed as a debt against the hen's own skeleton.
- 149 s of slow narration in a 9:16 frame is a long-form rhythm pasted into Shorts.

**Our move**
Open on the finished egg, cut the five-organ tour to two named stations, and spend the back half on the clock and the calcium debt. The slow tour is replaced by a countdown.

- **twenty-five**: say as one phrase, “twen-tee FIVE”, not “two five”.
- **eighteen to twenty**: full words, no “18–20”, no “eighteen-twenty”.
- **calcium**: KAL-see-um, hard C.
- **oviduct** appears only on screen, never spoken.
- **magnum**, **medullary**, **isthmus** appear only in cues and on-screen labels; the VO keeps 6th-grade vocabulary.
- Final line must be read with the same inflection as line 1 so the loop is seamless; no upward lift at the end.

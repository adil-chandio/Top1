- **Language:** English (VO) + burned-in kinetic English captions (1-3 words)
- **Tone:** Hypnotic, cinematic, mystery-reveal, science-grounded

## VO Script (final)

[HOOK / 0-2s]
This rock holds two animals.

[SETUP]
Two hundred fifty million years ago, one curled up asleep.

It was a mammal relative, beside an amphibian.

[TWIST_BEAT]
Wait. They were not enemies.

[EVIDENCE]
The amphibian's ribs were healing, with no bite marks.

So this was not a hunt.

[REVEAL]
The likely story: it crawled in for shelter.

The other was probably in aestivation, a deep dormancy.

[PAYOFF]
Then a flood filled the burrow and sealed them.

That is the clue the rock kept.

[LOOP/END]

01 / KIT OUTLINE

From topic to timeline.

Six planned steps that build on each other. The free sample shows the script and shot planning step.

  1. 01

    Find the angle

    Pick one audience and one takeaway. Narrow a broad topic to a promise you can cover in a Short.

  2. 02

    Write the hook

    Compare three openings and choose one you can show visually in the first few seconds.

  3. 03

    Script and storyboard

    Turn the hook into a three-beat voiceover with timestamps, captions, and simple shots.

  4. 04

    Prompt the visuals

    Write a prompt per shot with a subject, framing, motion, and a shared visual style.

  5. 05

    Plan captions and cover

    Extract readable captions and draft a cover with a clear subject and short title.

  6. 06

    Check before publishing

    Review timing, factual claims, on-screen legibility, and asset permissions before you edit.

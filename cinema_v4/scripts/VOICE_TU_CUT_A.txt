Yeh duniya tujhe lectures degi. Yeh kar. Yeh mat kar.
Lekin in me se koi ek bhi aake tujhe yeh nahi bolega — tu yeh kar, main tere sath khada hoon. Koi nahi bolta.
Kyunki bolne se kisi ka kuch nahi jaata. Karne se jaata hai.
Socho — tum bahar se aate ho. Ghar me aag lagi hai. Ammi. Biwi. Bachay. Andar.
Kya tum wahin khade sochoge — mujhse hoga ya nahi? risk zyada hai, main quit kar deta hoon? Nahi. Sawaal hi paida nahi hota.
Kyunki wahan Do or Die hai.
To aaj ek baat ka jawab do — jab unki jaan ke liye apni poori zindagi daao par laga sakte ho, to apne aur unke future ke liye khud ke kuch saal kurbaan nahi kar sakte?
Aur agar us aag me tumne quit nahi kiya, to aaj kyun?
Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon.

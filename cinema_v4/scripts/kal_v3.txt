Ustaad ne bola, kal aa jana.

Teen din ho gaye. Gari wahi khadi hai, usi kone me, wahi kapda uske upar.

Woh jhoot nahi bolta. Woh jaanta hai ke tu kal aayega. Kyunki tu har cheez ke liye kal aata hai.

Kaam kal. Call kal. Maafi kal. Ghar jaana kal.

Aur beech me ek din aisa aata hai jab woh kisi aur ki gari utha leta hai. Teri gari ko side pe khiska deta hai. Aur phir wohi kehta hai — bhai, kal aa jana.

Aur jab tu kal aata hai, gari wahi hoti hai, kaam wahi hota hai, aur tu bhi wahi hota hai. Sirf ek cheez badalti hai. Tera ek din kam ho jata hai.

Ustaad ke paas teri gari hai. Tere paas kya hai? Ek kal.

Sawaal yeh nahi ke gari kab ready hogi. Sawaal yeh hai ke tu kab ready hoga?

Woh kal kabhi nahi aata. Isliye aaj aa.

Teen sau card. Paanch naam.

Abbu ne shaadi ke teen sau card likhe, aur beta paas baith kar unko ginta raha tha. Beta ko sirf paanch naam chahiye the, un logon ke jo sach me apne hain.

Beta ne poocha: "in card pe naam kya likhun?" Abbu ne kaha: "log hain, naam nahi."

Beta ne kaha: "to paanch naam batao. Sirf paanch."

Abbu hanse, phir pencil uthai, aur pehla naam likhne se pehle pencil hawa me ruk gayi.

Do naam aaye, teesra aaya hi nahi, kyunki teesre naam ke saath ek purani ladaai yaad aa gayi jo unhone kabhi nahi ladi thi.

Teen sau card, aur paanch naam poore nahi hue, aur beta kuch nahi bola.

Abbu ne kaha: "sab log aayenge, aur khane ki ginti bhi teen sau hai."

Beta ne poocha: "jab tum bimaar the, tab kaun aaya tha?" Abbu ne jawab nahi diya, lekin unki ungli card ki ginti par wapas chali gayi.

Phir unhone aakhri card uthaya, aur us pe do lafz likhe jo kisi ka naam nahi the.

Beta ne dekha. Us card pe likha tha, "log nahi."

Aur woh card kisi aur card ke neeche chala gaya, jahan kisi ko nazar nahi aata.

Teen sau card, teen sau plate, aur ek aadmi ke paas paanch naam nahi.

Jinke naam tum nahi le sakte, unke naam pe tum apni zindagi nahi de sakte.

Yeh duniya tujhe lectures degi. Yeh kar. Yeh mat kar.
Lekin in me se koi ek bhi aake tujhe yeh nahi bolega — tu yeh kar, main tere sath khada hoon. Koi nahi bolta.
Kyunki bolne se kisi ka kuch nahi jaata. Karne se jaata hai.
Socho — tum bahar se aate ho. Ghar me aag lagi hai. Ammi. Biwi. Bachay. Andar.
Kya tum wahin khade sochoge — mujhse hoga ya nahi? risk zyada hai, main quit kar deta hoon? Nahi. Sawaal hi paida nahi hota.
Kyunki wahan Do or Die hai.
To aaj ek baat ka jawab do — jab unki jaan ke liye apni poori zindagi daao par laga sakte ho, to apne aur unke future ke liye khud ke kuch saal kurbaan nahi kar sakte?
Aur agar us aag me tumne quit nahi kiya, to aaj kyun?
Imagine kar — 10 saal baad tera beta ya beti tujhse poochti hai: sab relatives ke paas achi gariyan hain, acha ghar hai, hamare paas kyun nahi?
To kya jawab dega? Beta, tumhare papa ke paas time bohat tha. Papa chahte to bohat kuch kar sakte the. Lekin papa snaps bana rahe the. Scrolling kar rahe the.
Aur yeh duniya kitni khubsurat hai na, koi bhookh se mar jaata hai, apnon se madad maang maang kar thak jaata hai, aur akhir me dum tor deta hai. Phir wohi apne aate hain, aur mayyat par khane ka intezam karte hain.
Ek andha aadmi tha. Uska ek hi sahara tha, uska danda. Phir aankhon ka ilaj hua, aur kamyab bhi hua. Aur usne sab se pehle apne dande ko kachre me phenk diya.
Aur yeh baat dande ke liye nahi thi.
Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon.

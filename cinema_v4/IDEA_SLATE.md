# 1. `WAKEEL` — Tumhara dimaag tumhara dost nahi, wakeel hai

**Thesis (screenshot line):**
> *"Tumhara dimaag tumhara dost nahi hai. Woh tumhara wakeel hai — aur usne aaj tak ek hi case jeeta hai: tumhara."*

**Hook (0–2s):** *"Tum apne dimaag ko dost samajh rahe ho. Woh tumhara wakeel hai."*

**Mechanism:** Dimaag ka kaam sach nahi, **insaan ko bachana** hai. Isliye har failure par woh ek defence banata hai: "thake the", "time nahi tha", "log aise hi hote hain", "iski kismat achi thi". Har case **acquit** ho jaata hai. Tum barree ho jaate ho. Aur **wahi jail** me rehte ho.

**Image:** Khoopri ke andar courtroom — x-ray benches, exhibits me tumhari excuses, judge ki jagah ek khaali kursi, verdict: *"kal se."*

**Turn:** *"Tum har case jeet rahe ho. Isi liye tum abhi bhi andar ho."*

**Payoff / brand reframe:** *"Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon"* ka naya matlab — *apni taraf* hona **apna bachav** karna nahi, **apne aap se sach** bolna hai.

**Risk:** thoda intellectual — lekin visual courtroom ise massy bana deta hai.

---

# 2. `CHHOTA` — Tum khud ko chhota rakh rahe ho, taake koi door na jaye

**Thesis:**
> *"Tum apni roshni kam kar rahe ho — taake kisi ko andhera na lage."*

**Hook:** *"Tum ne apna plan isliye nahi chhoda, kyunki tumhe lagta hai bada hona akela hona hai."*

**Mechanism:** Jab tum bade hote ho, log **chhoot** jaate hain. Isliye dimaag chupke se tumhe chhota rakhta hai — "itna kaafi hai", "sab aise hi hain". Ye **aaram** nahi, **khud ko bachane** ka tareeqa hai. Aur tum uske badle apni zindagi de rahe ho.

**Image:** Ek aadmi bade logon ke kamre me dheere dheere chhota hota hua — deewarein oonchi, darwaza door. Phir scale palat jaata hai.

**Turn:** *"Jo tumse sach me pyaar karte hain, unhe tumhara bada hona dara nahi sakta. Baqi ka case alag hai."*

**Payoff:** *"Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon"* = maine chhota hona band kar diya — aur koi chala nahi gaya.

**Risk:** Rishtedaari/parivaar ko chhuye ga — comments me bahas, jo algorithm ko chahiye.

# 3. `KAL WALA INSAAN` — Tum "kal" ko ek insaan samajhte ho

**Thesis:**
> *"Tumhara 'kal' koi din nahi hai. Woh ek insaan hai — jise tumne bana diya, jo tumhara kaam karega. Woh kabhi nahi aata."*

**Hook:** *"Tum 'kal' ko ek insaan samajh rahe ho. Woh insaan nahi hai."*

**Mechanism:** "Kal se" bolte waqt dimaag me ek **chehra** hota hai — zyada energetic, zyada himmat wala, zyada free. Tum aaj ka bojh usko de dete ho. Woh aadmi **maujood nahi**. Aur jo maujood hai (tum, thake hue), uske paas pichhle teen saal ka poora bojh hai.

**Image:** Ek khali kursi, saamne chai ka cup, darwaza — koi aata nahi. Aakhri shot me us kursi par **tum** baith jaate ho, thorre bare, thorre thake.

**Turn:** *"Kal ka koi chehra nahi hai. Woh tum ho — bas woh tum, jo abhi bhi intezaar me hai."*

**Payoff:** ek chhota act — "kal" ka naam badal do: *10:00*.

**Risk:** concept bilkul fresh hai — copy karna mushkil, isliye signature banega.

# 4. `TRAILER` — Tum ek film ka trailer 100 baar dekh chuke ho

**Thesis:**
> *"Tum apni zindagi ki film ek baar bhi nahi dekh paye. Trailer sau baar dekh liya."*

**Hook:** *"Tum plan banane ke nashay me ho. Kaam karne ke nahi."*

**Mechanism:** Plan banane par dopamine milta hai — **anticipation** wala, result se pehle. Isliye tumne 40 plans banaye, 0 shuru kiye. Tumhe *kaam ki feeling* pasand hai, kaam nahi. Yeh nasha hai — aur ye legal hai, isliye kisi ne roka nahi.

**Image:** Ek khaali cinema hall, screen par **tumhara hi trailer** — jisme tum jeet rahe ho. Hall me ek hi shakhs: tum. Peeche se awaaz: *"ab film shuru karein?"*

**Turn:** *"Trailer tumhe kabhi woh feeling nahi degi jo film deti hai. Isliye tum 100 baar dekh rahe ho."*

**Payoff:** 10 minute, no plan — sirf pehla frame.

**Risk:** "nasha" lafz sensitive ho sakta hai — "aadat" kar denge.

---

# 5. `SABAR` (provocative, high-risk high-reward) — Tumhara sabar tumhara dard-chhupane ka tareeqa hai

**Thesis:**
> *"Tum sabar nahi kar rahe. Tum apne aap ko chup karwa rahe ho — aur uska naam sabar rakh diya hai."*

**Hook:** *"Tumhe sabar samjha gaya hai. Woh sabar nahi tha."*

**Mechanism:** Asli sabar = jab tum koshish kar rahe ho. Jo tum kar rahe ho = **rukna**, aur usko sabar kehna. Isse gussa khatam ho jaata hai, dard khatam ho jaata hai, **zindagi** bhi.

**Turn:** *"Sabar me aawaz hoti hai. Chuppi me nahi."*

**Payoff:** ek line — "jo tumne aaj se seh liya, woh kal tumse le liya jayega."

**Risk:** bohat high — kuch log naraz honge, lekin comments/argument explode karega. Sirf tab chuno jab bahas chahiye ho.

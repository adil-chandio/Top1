# 2. Kyun ye chalta hai (device by device)

| Device | Kahan | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
**Anaphora** | "Kal se. Kal pakka. Kal zaroor." | Teen baar, teesri par wazan — pehli 3 second me hi |

**Object** | **khaali kursi** | Poori film ka ek hi visual — retell hone wali image |
**Accounting** | "thora thora… badle me ek aur kal" | Emotion nahi, **hisaab** |

**Trap close** | "tu kis ka intezaar kar raha hai?" | Koi jawab maujood nahi — **verdict** |
**Signature sound** | silence → POP → whisper | Brand sound, har episode me |
**Brand reframe** | "Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon" | Ab matlab: *kal* se nahi, **aaj** se apni taraf |

# 4. Delivery direction (voice)

- **B1:** flat, thaka hua. Jaise hisaab likh rahe ho. "Kal zaroor" par 200 ms rukna.
- **B2–B3:** dheere dheere kadwahat. "Jise **tune khud banaya**" par zor.
- **B4:** "thora thora" — **dheema** karo, jaise ginti kar rahe ho. "Ek aur kal" par 400 ms pehle khamoshi.
- **B5:** naram, dard bhara. "chai thandi hoti rehti hai" — **bilkul flat**, koi acting nahi.
- **B6:** sab se dheema beat. "woh tu hi tha" — **phusphusa kar**.
- **B7:** seedha, bina gussa. Sawaal, farmaan nahi. Aakhir me saans.
- **B8:** POP ke baad **800 ms kuch nahi**, phir whisper.

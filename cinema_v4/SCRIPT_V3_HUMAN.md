# 2. NAYA RESEARCH — 3 cheezein jo sab badal deti hain

### 2.1 Platform khud "raw" maang raha hai (2026)
Adam Mosseri ne 2026 ko **"Year of Raw Content"** declare kiya. Instagram ne Reels Insights me **"share and skip rate"** add kiya — agar viewer ko lage video **overproduced ya AI-generated** hai, woh skip karta hai, aur high skip rate **reach maar deta hai**. Reports: phone-shot authentic video polished content se **500–800% zyada engagement**, 85% Gen Z lo-fi ko prefer karte hain.
**Matlab:** hamara "premium cinema" ka plan **dikhne me** ad jaisa nahi hona chahiye. Craft **invisible** honi chahiye.

### 2.2 Slop ke structural tells (GitHub lexicon)
`slop.json` sirf lafz nahi pakadta, **shakal** pakadta hai:
`"not just X, it's Y"` · `rule-of-three triad` · `one-word rhetorical question line` · `video preamble` · `three+ shouted words` · `reflex follow/tag bait` · `emoji bullets` · `hashtag wall`.
> **Rule:** insaan teen cheezein ek saath nahi kehta. Insaan **ek** cheez kehta hai, phir ruk kar doosri.

### 2.3 Show, don't tell (craft ka asli mechanism)
- "Woh nervous tha" = **tell**. "Usne apni shirt ka collar teen baar theek kiya" = **show**.
- **Specific unlocks universal** — jo detail sirf aapki lagti hai, wahi sab ki lagti hai.
- Emotion label mat do, **behaviour** do. Reader khud nateeja nikale — usi "inference" me feeling paida hoti hai.
- **Tiny details** = intimacy: chipped mug, charger, 2 baje ki screen, wohi file.

# 3. Asli tension aur uska hal

> **Premium ≠ polished-looking.** Premium = **beautiful light + real texture + thoda imperfect framing**. A24, not Apple ad.

| Layer | Kaisa |
|---|---|
Camera | cinematic (god rays, DOF, grade) — **lekin** micro-handheld drift, frame kabhi bilkul centered nahi |
Performance | **asli insaan ki awaaz** — saans sunai de, "…" ho, jaldi na kare |
Grade | warm skin, real grain, thodi si kick — **clean CGI shine nahi** |
Sound | room tone, cloth, saans, door ka shor — **music ke neeche insaan ki layer** |
Writing | pehla person, apni galti, chhoti detail, koi slogan nahi |

# 8. SAB SE ZAROORI — apni awaaz

Ye script TTS me bhi achi lagegi. Lekin **sach ye hai: koi synthetic awaaz "raw content" ka test nahi pass karti.** Mosseri ka shift + skip-rate metric ke baad, **aapki apni awaaz 10x behtar** perform karegi — kyunki is script ka poora point **confession** hai, aur confession **kisi ki apni awaaz** maangta hai.

Recording (phone se, 3 minute):
- Kamra: kapde wala, chhota, carpet — bathroom/almari ke paas sab se behtar (echo khatam)
- Phone 15–20 cm, thoda side me (seedha saamne nahi — "plosive" kam)
- **3 takes**: (1) normal, (2) thoda dheema, (3) jaise kisi dost ko bata rahe ho — teesra sab se behtar hota hai
- Music/effects **baad me** — pehle sirf awaaz
- Galti, saans, "…" — **mat kaato**. Wohi human touch hai.

Mujhe WAV de dijiye, main usko cleanup (denoise, EQ, -14 LUFS) karke video me laga dunga.
Agar aap chahte hain ke main voice-06 se generate karun, woh bhi ready hai — lekin ye meri honest recommendation hai.

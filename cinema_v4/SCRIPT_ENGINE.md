### Unki 3 laws jo sab se zyada matter karti hain
1. **"Ek Reel ke do hooks hote hain, aur woh ek hi jumla nahi hota"** — jo tum **bolte** ho, aur jo **screen par** likha hai. Spoken hook ko ~2 second, on-screen ko ~1 second.
2. **"Raw views evidence nahi hain."** Asli evidence = **outlier multiple** = `views ÷ us account ka apna recent median`. **>3x = signal**, <1.5x = normal din. Aur accounts **apne size ke 10x andar** dekho — 2M-follower formula 2M ki wajah se chalta hai.
3. **"A script in the wrong voice is unusable, because they have to say it out loud."**

### Web — structure + psychology
- Reel shape: `0–2s HOOK → 2–7s STAKE → body one idea/beat → last 3s PAYOFF → last line LOOP` (hook ka ek lafz echo karta hai). Length **15–45s**; hook **<3s**; koi beat **>4s** nahi; **loop mandatory**.
- **Curiosity gap** (Loewenstein's information-gap theory): dopamine *anticipation* me nikalta hai, answer me nahi. Isliye loop ko **manageable** rakho — band mat karo, choke mat karo.
- **Hook first second = 41% higher retention**; first 3s me humor = **51% more shares**; cliff at 3s = hook weak; mid-video dip = open loop missing.
- **Bait-and-switch = algorithmic suppression** (early drop-off signal).
- **Milton Model** (Erickson): presupposition, embedded command, double bind, conversational postulate, pacing-then-leading, negative command — yahi "hypnotic" ka actual mechanism hai, vague hype nahi.

# 2. VIRAL SCRIPT DNA — 10 laws

| # | Law | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
1 | **Do hook, ek waqt** | Bola hua ≠ screen par likha. Dono alag likho |
2 | **Pehle 2 second = poora faisla** | Frame 0 par motion, chehra, aur sawaal |
3 | **Stake 5 second me** | "Yeh tumhare baare me hai" — warna viewer chala gaya |
4 | **Ek beat = ek idea** | Har beat par frame badle. Do ideas = zero ideas |
5 | **Specific > poetic** | "Teen saal" > "bohat arsa". Numbers trust banate hain |
6 | **Abstraction sirf identity line me** | Facts specific, identity open (taake viewer khud ko dekh sake) |
7 | **Ek open loop poore video me** | Question pehle 3s me khula, aakhir me band — aur naya khula |
8 | **Contrast = retention** | "Aaram, ya haar?" — do lafzon me tension |
9 | **Turn (insight) lazmi** | Woh line jo viewer ne socha nahi tha. Iske bina video "motivation" hai, "revelation" nahi |
10 | **Loop echo** | Aakhri line hook ka ek lafz wapas laaye → replay "decision" nahi lagta |

### 2.1 Anti-cliché list (yeh lafz BANNED — 100M reels ne khatam kar diye)
`sapne dekho` · `mehnat karo` · `manzil` · `safar` · `kamyabi` · `positive socho` · `never give up` · `zindagi ek test hai` · `khud par bharosa rakho`
> In lafzon ka matlab galat nahi — **in par claim khatam ho chuka hai**. Differentiation **zubaan** se aati hai, message se nahi.

### 2.2 "Hypnotized" ka asli mechanism (Milton patterns, honestly used)
| Pattern | Kaise | EP5 example |
|---|---|---|
**Presupposition** | Sach maan kar aage badho | *"Jab das minute poore hon…"* — maan liya ke honge |
**Embedded command** | Jumle ke andar hukam | *"…khud ko woh purana waada yaad dilana"* |
**Double bind** | Do options, dono manzil tak | *"Aaram, ya haar?"* |
**Conversational postulate** | Sawaal jo kaam karwa de | *"Kisi ne poocha?"* |
**Negative command** | "Mat karo" — dhyan pakadta hai | *"uska naam sukoon mat rakhna"* |
**Pacing → leading** | Pehle sach bolo, phir le chalo | *"Tum thake hue nahi ho…"* (pace) → *"…tum naraz ho"* (lead) |

### 4.1 VO direction (voice-06)
- **B1:** flat, low, no emotion — jaise fact bata rahe ho. "teen saal" par 120 ms rukna.
- **B2:** thoda tez, seedha viewer ko.
- **B3:** list = teen alag alag pitch, har slab par. "Sirf chup" — **whisper**, 400 ms silence.
- **B4:** warm, phir thanda (sukoon ka mazaq).
- **B5:** **slowest beat**. "Tum thake hue nahi ho" ke baad 300 ms pause. Ye video ka dil hai.
- **B6:** triple rhythm, machine-gun, chhote gaps.
- **B7:** instruction tone — clear, koi poetry nahi.
- **B8:** pehla jumla dheema (identity), aakhri sawaal **seedha, bina melody**.

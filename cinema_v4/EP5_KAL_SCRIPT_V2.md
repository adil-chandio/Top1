## 1. THE SCRIPT — verbatim

> **Line breaks are breath, not paragraphs.** Read it out loud once before you judge it.

```
Woh aadmi aaj bhi nahi aaya.

Kal se. Kal pakka. Kal zaroor. Roz yehi. Aur woh kabhi nahi aata.

Ghar me ek mask pada hai. Kaala, do aankhein, laal roshni. Tune woh mask us aadmi ke liye banwaya jo abhi tak nikla hi nahi.

Uske naam par tu ek din deta hai. Phir ek din. Phir ek din. Aur badle me woh tujhe kya deta hai? Ek aur kal.

Ek din tu woh mask uthata hai, phone ka camera kholta hai, aur screen me jo aadmi dikhta hai woh wohi hai — jo kabhi aaya hi nahi.

Kyunki woh bahar nahi tha. Woh tu tha. Bas kal ke naam par baitha hua, aur us din ka intezaar karta hua jo kabhi nahi aata.

Sawaal yeh nahi ke woh kab aayega. Sawaal yeh hai — kab tu uski jagah khada hoga?

Main abhi bhi apni taraf hoon.
```

**144 words · 8 beats · 45 s.** Every noun is filmable: *mask, aankhein, roshni, phone, camera, screen, ghar.*

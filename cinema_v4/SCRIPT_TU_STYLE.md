# 1. Aapki script me kya kaam kar raha hai (10 devices)

Aapne jo likha, woh **motivation nahi — indictment** hai. Isliye chubhta hai. Devices:

| # | Device | Aapki line |
|---|---|---|
**1** | **Duniya ko mulzim banayo, viewer ko nahi** | *"Yeh duniya tujhe lectures degi — yeh kar, yeh mat kar."* Viewer attack me nahi, **defence** me mehsoos karta hai |
**2** | **Anaphora (jaan boojh kar dohrana)** | *"tu yeh kar… tu yeh kar… tu yeh kar…"* — teesri baar par wazan badh jaata hai |
**3** | **Khamoshi ka hisaab (cost ledger)** | *"is zabaan se kuch bahir nikaalne se kisi ka kuch nahi jaata — aur karne se sab ka jaata hai."* Ye line **economics** hai, feel-good nahi |
**4** | **Future-pacing, chehre ke saath** | *"10 saal baad tera beta ya beti poochti hai…"* — guilt ko ek **gawah** mil jaata hai |
**5** | **Kaday sach jo koi nahi bolta** | *"unke wohi apne aate hain aur mayyat par khane ka intezam karte hain."* Ye line video ka **"wtf"** hai |
**6** | **Tamseel (parable) jo aaine ki tarah kaam kare** | andha aadmi + danda, aur phir kicker: *"yeh baat dande ke liye nahi thi."* |
**7** | **Counterfactual se sabit karna** | aag wala scene — "mujhse hoga?" ka sawaal **khatam** kar deta hai. Capacity **sabit** ho jaati hai |
**8** | **Do-or-Die substitution** | *"wahan situation Do or Die hai"* — excuse ki jagah nahi bachti |
**9** | **Logic ka jaal (trap close)** | *"agar us situation me quit nahi kiya — to aaj kaise kar sakte ho?"* Iska koi jawab maujood nahi. Ye **verdict** hai |
**10** | **Zero abstraction** | kamyabi/manzil/safar **ek lafz nahi**. Sirf: gari, ghar, snaps, khana, danda, kachra, **aag** |

**Sab se important:** aap likhte waqt **kisi ko bachane** ki koshish nahi kar rahe — aap **hisaab** maang rahe ho. Isliye sunne wala baith jaata hai.

> Mere pichhle scripts "smart" thay. Aapka script **sach** hai. Isliye ab format aapka hoga, sirf **timing aur shots** meri taraf se.

**Pop ka design:** 6 frame pressure riser (ultra low, almost inaudible) → **0.0s** → balloon pop + room reverb → 800 ms kuch nahi → whisper. Ye sequence **signature** ban jayegi (har episode me aakhir me).

# 6. Visual ka ek hi rule

Har shot me **do hi cheezein** dikhengi: **aadmi** aur **uska faisla**. Koi decorative 3D building nahi, koi "environment flex" nahi.
Aag, danda, kachra, mayyat ki plate, phone ki roshni, aankhein — **bas ye**.
Yeh aapki script ka tone hai: **spartan, gussa, sach**.

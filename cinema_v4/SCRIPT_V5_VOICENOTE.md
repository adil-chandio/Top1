# 2. Research — asli fix

### 2.1 Jo technique literally yehi problem solve karti hai
> *"A common trick among experienced script writers is to **record themselves explaining the topic without notes, transcribe it, and edit that transcript into the script** rather than starting from a blank page. The result almost always sounds more natural than a script drafted directly in formal prose."*
> — script-writing craft guide

Main aapko record nahi kar sakta, **lekin main transcript register me likh sakta hoon** — jaise kisi ne voice note bheja ho aur usko sirf saaf kiya gaya ho.

### 2.2 Compression, transcription nahi
Real speech me: **repeats, adhure jumle, false starts, filler** hote hain. Unki advice: *"think compression, not transcription — borrow the rhythm and small imperfections of real talk but strip out dead weight."*
Matlab: **1–2 stumbles per 40 words** (zyada = nasamajh, kam = robot). Aapki script me **jaan boojh kar** daalne hain.

### 2.3 TTS ke liye exact mechanics (agar synthetic use ho)
- **False start:** pehla lafz dohrao, dash, space → `"Main- main teen saal se..."`
- **Filler:** plain lafz, comma ke darmiyan → `"yaar,"` `"matlab,"` `"dekho,"`
- **Trailing off:** `"aur phir..."`
- **Rule:** ek reply/clip me **ek** stumble — stack mat karo.
- **Important:** "detail jo point hai" me stumble **nahi** — numbers, naam saaf rahenge.

### 2.4 Comedy craft = specificity + callback
- *"I flew home"* weak. *"I paid forty dollars to be patted down by a man named Chuck at 5 AM"* — specific.
- **Callback:** pehle plant karo, baad me wapas lao — audience ko "insider" feel hota hai, aur wohi **loop** banta hai.
- Emotion pehle, punchline baad me.

# 5. Ab asli masla: awaaz

Ye script text me insaan lagti hai. Lekin **TTS isko insaan nahi banayegi** — kyunki robot ka tell lafz nahi, **prosody** hai: har jumla barabar lambai ka, saans nahi, "chuckle" nahi, jaldi/der se bolna nahi.

| | Aapki awaaz (phone) | voice-06 (TTS) |
|---|---|---|
False start | asli | nakli lagta hai |
Chuckle | asli | nahi ho sakta |
Saans | asli | nahi |
"Yaar" ka tone | asli | flat |
Skip-rate test (2026) | pass | **fail** |

**Isliye:** ye script **aapki awaaz** ke liye likhi gayi hai. 3 minute ka kaam hai:

1. **Kamra:** kapde/almari wala chhota room (echo khatam). Raat, jab ghar quiet ho.
2. **Phone:** 15–20 cm, thoda side, aawaz **dheemi** — chillana nahi.
3. **3 takes:** (a) normal, (b) dheema, (c) **jaise kisi dost ko raat 2 baje bata rahe ho**. Teesra hi use hoga.
4. **Galtiyan mat kaato.** "main— main" aur "yaar" **rakhein**. Wohi human hai.
5. Bhej dein WAV/MP3. Main denoise + EQ + de-ess + -14 LUFS karunga, aur **aapke pauses par video time karunga** — machine par nahi.

Agar aap phir bhi TTS chahein, main voice-06 se **15-second test** bana sakta hoon, aap sun kar compare karein. Bas honest baat: video ka "robot" wala ehsas 90% awaaz se aata hai, script se nahi.

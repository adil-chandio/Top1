06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

What do I get from the builder?

A text prompt. Paste it into your AI chat to ask for a script, timed shots, captions, and visual directions. The shop doesn’t generate or export a video.

How were the 3D demos made?

They’re original 3D films made for Top1 Kits, cut with camera moves, visual effects, and an original sound mix. Each has a companion prompt brief. The builder helps you plan similar shots; making and exporting your own video takes a separate editor or renderer.

Can I use it from an Android phone?

Yes. Open the site in a current browser, build a prompt, then copy it or save the text. The prompts can ask for a script in English or Roman Urdu.

Do I need an AI subscription?

Not for this builder or the sample download. You use your own AI chat to run the prompt, and that service has its own access and usage limits.

Can I buy or send an order yet?

Not in this preview. You can view the kit, download the sample, and create a brief to copy or save. No payments or briefs are submitted here.

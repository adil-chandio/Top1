# 10 ghaltiyan jo bachni hain

Har ek ke saath: kyun galat hai, aur kya karna hai.

---

## 1. "YouTube automation" bechna

**Kyun galat:** YouTube ki inauthentic content policy (July 2025 rename, July 2026 clarification) template-based mass production ko monetize nahi karti. Jan 2026 mein 16 channels (35M subs) terminate hue. Jo seller "automation se paisa" bechta hai, uske buyer demonetize hote hain aur refund maangte hain.

**Kya karo:** "Original 3D explainer system" becho. Unique script + custom composition. Har doc mein policy link. Koi monetization guarantee nahi.

---

## 2. Agentic workflow 6 hafte mein bechne ki koshish

**Kyun galat:** `scenes.js` mein har film haath se likhi hai. Generator nahi hai. Layer ⑥ (scene assembly) banana 2–3 mahine ka kaam hai.

**Kya karo:** Pilot se shuru — 3 videos, saath mein banao. Pilot ke baghair koi $2,000 nahi dega. Pilot ke baad tumhe pata hoga kahan tootta hai.

---

## 3. Order lene ka raasta na hona

**Kyun galat:** Site par aaj **koi email, form ya contact nahi** hai. Promotion karoge to views aayenge, order nahi.

**Kya karo:** **Aaj** ek email + brief form. Ye 1 ghante ka kaam hai aur sab kuch unlock karta hai.

---

## 4. "Har video 100% scratch se naya 3D hoga" bolna

**Kyun galat:** Sach nahi hai. Models library se aayenge. Client 3rd video par pakad lega, trust khatam.

**Kya karo:** "Har video ka script, edit aur composition naya. Models library se, jo har project ke saath badhti hai. Naya custom model alag charge." Ye bilkul respectable hai — asli studios aise hi kaam karte hain.

---

## 5. Free sample sabko dena

**Kyun galat:** 2 ghante per sample × 20 prospects = 40 ghante zaya. Aur free sample lene wale 90% kabhi nahi kharidte.

**Kya karo:** Sirf **pehle 10 qualified prospects** ko 5-second test. Baaki ko existing showreel clip.

---

## 6. "Exposure ke liye free" ya "baad mein pay"

**Kyun galat:** Exposure kisi ka bill nahi bharta. "Baad mein" = kabhi nahi. Aur ek free client permanent free client ban jata hai.

**Kya karo:** 50% advance, non-refundable script approval ke baad. Pehle 3 clients ke liye 50% discount — wo bhi case study ki condition par.

---

## 7. Render speed measure kiye baghair delivery promise

**Kyun galat:** Software WebGL slow hai. Agar ek 30s film 4 ghante leti hai, hafte mein 4–8 max. 5 clients ek hafte mein = quality crash + late delivery + refund.

**Kya karo:** `node services/tools/render-budget.mjs` chalao. Asli number pehle, phir promise.

---

## 8. Google Drive service account ko My Drive mein upload karne ki koshish

**Kyun galat:** Service accounts ka apna storage quota nahi hota. Upload fail hoga — shayad pilot ke din.

**Kya karo:** Workspace + Shared Drive, ya OAuth as user. Pehle din 1 KB test file se check karo.

---

## 9. Buyer ke account costs ko ignore karna

**Kyun galat:** LLM + TTS + render ka kharcha buyer ka hai. Agar ye pehle se clear nahi, 2 mahine baad "tumne bataya nahi tha" wala jhagra.

**Kya karo:** `automation/RUNNING-COSTS.md` ka copy contract aur onboarding doc dono mein daalo.

---

## 10. Teen offers ek saath launch karna

**Kyun galat:** Dhyaan bat jayega, kuch bhi theek se nahi hoga. Aur Offer C ke bina proof, wo bekar bik jayega.

**Kya karo:** Order: **A abhi** (proof ready) → **B 3–4 hafte** → **C 3–4 mahine**. Ek waqt par ek cheez.

---

## Bonus — 3 chhoti ghaltiyan

**12. Har post par price likhna.** Price DM mein. Post par price likhne se negotiation comments mein chali jati hai.

**13. Bulk DM karna.** Instagram account ban ho jayega. 10/day, har ek personalized.

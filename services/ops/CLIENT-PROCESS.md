# Client Process — pehli inquiry se delivery tak

## Day 0 — Inquiry

**Reply 2 ghante mein.** 2 ghante ke andar jawab = 5× zyada conversion.

Template `../offers/OFFER-A-3D-VIDEO.md` mein hai.

**Jo kabhi nahi karna:**
- Price se pehle scope discuss karna (scope se pehle price mat batao, ya range batao)
- "Aap batao kitna denge" — aap price bolte ho
- Scope ke baghair "haan ban jayega" bolna

---

## Day 0–1 — Brief

5 sawaal ka form. Jawab aane par:

```
[ ] Budget match karta hai?
[ ] Timeline realistic hai? (rush = +40%)
[ ] Assets mile hain? (logo, product photos, voice file)
[ ] Koi copyright risk? (unka footage/models)
[ ] Kya ye policy-safe hai? (health/finance claims = extra care)
```

**Green flag:** clear product, reference hai, budget theek, timeline 1 hafte+
**Red flag:** "bas jaldi chahiye", reference nahi, 10 revision maangta hai, budget nahi batata

---

## Day 1 — Proposal + advance

```
1. Written scope bhejo (kya hai, kya nahi hai) — 1 page, PDF
2. Price + timeline
3. 50% advance invoice
4. Advance aane par hi shuru
```

**Scope document mein ye 3 cheezein bold:**
- Revisions: 2 (script 1, picture 1)
- Raw files included nahi
- Upload/views ka scope nahi

---

## Day 1–2 — Script

- 3 beats, VO script (word budget ke andar)
- Client ko **approval** chahiye, "dekh lo" nahi
- Approval ke baghair aage nahi

**Revision gate 1.** Script approve hone ke baad scope change = naya charge.

---

## Day 2–3 — Still approval ⭐

**Ye step skip mat karo.**

Ek single frame render karo (highest quality). Client ko bhejo:
```
"Ye ek frame hai poori film ka look — lighting, color, camera.
Ismein change chahiye to ab batao. Iske baad poora render isi look par hoga."
```

10 minute ka approval, 3 din ka galat render bachata hai.

---

## Day 3–6 — Render

- Progress update Day 4 aur Day 5 par (agar 7 din se zyada)
- Watermarked preview banao (lower-third text "PREVIEW")
- Client ko preview + 1 revision ka mauqa

**Revision gate 2.**

---

## Day 7 — Delivery

```
final.mp4        (1080×1920, H.264, faststart)
poster.webp
captions.srt
qa-report.json   ← ye aapka proof hai
```

Delivery message:
```
[naam] bhai, ho gaya. Files neeche.

- final.mp4 — 1080×1920, 24fps, 29s
- poster.webp — thumbnail
- captions.srt — YouTube/Instagram upload ke liye
- qa-report.json — technical verification (duration, loudness, decode)

Note: main YouTube upload nahi karta — wo aap khud karo.
Agar koi technical issue ho to 7 din tak batao, fix kar dunga.

Case study ke liye 2 line feedback de dena — bahut madad hogi.

[naam]
```

**50% final payment release se pehle.** Watermarked preview tak, phir payment, phir final files.

---

## Delivery ke baad (ye 80% log nahi karte)

**Day 8:** "Video kaisa laga?" + testimonial maango
**Day 15:** "Koi aur video chahiye?" (repeat client = 3× profit)
**Day 30:** Case study publish karo (permission ke saath)

**Referral:** "Kisi ko zaroorat ho to bata dena — aapke reference par 10% off milega."

---

## Red flags — kaam chhod do

- Advance nahi de raha par "kaam shuru karo"
- Scope roz badalta hai
- "Baad mein pay karunga, video se paisa aayega"
- Apni guarantee maang raha hai (views/monetization)
- Copyright wala footage/models de raha hai
- Abusive ya disrespectful

**Ek bhi red flag = politely decline.** Ek bure client ne 3 acche clients ka time kha jata hai.

---

## Time tracking (khud ke liye)

Har project ke baad:
```
client, tier, actual_hours, render_hours, revisions, paid_amount
```
3 projects ke baad aapko pata chal jayega aapka asli hourly rate kya hai. Tabhi price badhao.

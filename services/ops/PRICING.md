# Pricing — sochne ka tareeqa

## Formula

```
Price = (render_hours × your_hourly_rate) + revision_buffer + margin
```

Aapka hourly rate: Pakistan mein 3D artist **PKR 1,500–4,000/hr**. Start 1,500 par, 5 clients ke baad 2,500.

**Example — 30s film:**
```
Render + design time:   6 hours
Revision buffer 30%:    2 hours
Total:                  8 hours × PKR 1,500 = PKR 12,000
Margin 2.5×:            = PKR 30,000
International (×0.0060): ≈ $180
```

**Yehi math hai.** `node services/tools/render-budget.mjs` se apna asli render time daalo.

## Discount rules (tight rakho)

- ✅ Pehle 3 clients: 50% off (case study ke badle)
- ✅ 3+ videos ek saath: 15%
- ✅ Non-profit / student: 20%
- ❌ "Exposure ke liye free" — **kabhi nahi**
- ❌ "Baad mein pay karunga" — **kabhi nahi**
- ❌ Repeat client ko automatic discount — nahi, price badhta hai

---

## Negotiation — 3 jawab

**"Bahut mehnga hai"**
> "Samajh sakta hoon. Ye 6 ghante ka 3D kaam hai — 700 frames, custom lighting, voiceover, captions. Aap chahein to 15 second ka tier le lein, wo 25,000 ka hai. Quality wahi rahegi, sirf length kam."

**"AI se to free mein ban jata hai"**
> "Sahi kaha — AI se ban jata hai. Par AI 3D mechanism render nahi kar sakta, na hi shot timing lock kar sakta hai. Mera kaam 700 frames ka hai, jise main verify karke deta hoon. AI template se YouTube demonetize bhi kar deta hai."

**"Pehle sample do"**
> "Ek 5-second test shot free bana deta hoon aapke product ka. Agar pasand aaye to poora kaam. Ye fair hai?"
